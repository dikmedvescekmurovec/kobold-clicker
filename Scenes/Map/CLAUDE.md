## Map code (`Scenes/Map/`)
A procedurally generated hex map, uncovered tile by tile: a hexagon of land ringed by an ice wall, with frozen wasteland past it. **Read `DESIGN.md` here before changing generation, saving or naming** -- it holds the why.

## Rules and gotchas
- **Widening `env_adjacency` (`terrain.ADJACENT`) changes `MapSave.fingerprint`, and every saved map would be refused:** add the old fingerprint to `WIDENED_SHEETS` against the new one. Narrowing it may not be done that way -- an old map could hold a border the new table forbids.
- **Generation rules come from the sheet's JSON meta** (`blend_rule`, `blend_priority`, `env_adjacency`) through `SheetMeta`: `HexTileset.can_border` and `EnvironmentGenerator.allowed()` are one table, so the generator cannot make a border the sprites cannot draw.
- **`TownWorld.RING`, `FIRST_RING` and `KEEP_OUT` are `MapBuilder.WALL_STEP`, `START_LAND_RADIUS` and `START_TOWN_DISTANCE`, held together by `test_generation`:** TownWorld may not name MapBuilder, which names it. Rings are counted in `WALL_STEP`s whatever the Ring of Walls says, like `Encounter.walls_inside`, so under that curse two of its six-wide rings share one fortress.
- **Map origins must be on even rows** (`MapBuilder` asserts it), or world neighbours stop matching on-screen ones.
- **Nothing generated is ever regenerated.** Both generators are order-dependent, not seed-dependent, so the same seed and rect do *not* give the same land. Land, roads, the town world and tile names are all written into the save; growing the map only ever adds.
- **A tile is named once, when it first comes out of the fog (`_show`), and the name is saved.** Do not recompute names on load.
- **`MapSave.load_from` has two nulls:** empty `problem` is a first run; a reason in `problem` is a refusal, and the caller must stop and never write over the file.
- **`MapSave` never names `MapBuilder`** (Godot's cycle checker): states are raw ints there, with `STATE_NAMES` as the legend.
- **Drawing a whole map goes through `_draw_saved`, not `_show`:** `set_ground` refreshes seven cells per call.
- **`MapBuilder.create` clears towns within `START_TOWN_DISTANCE` (5) of the centre and guarantees a small one exactly that far out,** linked by road to cell (0, 0). `restore` does none of that: the saved world already has it.
- **A settlement is two pictures:** its ground (clearing, plate, shadows) is the `town_<env>_<tier>` tile on GroundLayer, and its buildings are an 84x96 sprite on `HexMap.towns`, bigger than the hex and over the fog, so a town reads across the map and before the land round it is charted. `MapBuilder._draw_town` puts it down wherever a cell is drawn (`_show`, `_draw_saved`, a wall falling) and only on seen land -- none on the wall or past it. `TOWN_ANCHOR` is the generator's `towns.ANCHOR`.
- **Chests are derived, not saved:** `has_chest` is a per-cell roll (open land, `CHEST_MIN_DISTANCE` out) that holds until the tile is charted. A chest is drawn once seen, or anywhere under the dev's `Settings.show_all_chests()` (`redraw_chests` re-asks every cell). `nearest_chest()` scans every cell, so call it on arrival only, never per frame.
- **`envs_within` scans like `nearest_chest`:** ask it when a town is walked into, never per frame.
- **The fog gives way to gold in two places and nowhere else:** `scour` and the chest star, both the fortuneteller's.
- **`IceOverlay`'s sheet constants are the generator's** (`ice_wall.WASTE_COLS` / `WASTE_ROWS`, `VARIANTS`, `ACCENT_KINDS`, `ACCENT_CHANCE`): change one, change both and rerun `build_ice.py`.
- **Nothing past the wall is the player's until it falls.** Wasteland is seen as snow (no ground, no road, no fog veil, `name_of` is `WASTE_NAME`), never charted or walked to. A wall tile draws its real ground under the ice but reads as `WALL_NAME`, `area_variant` "plain" and `town_tier` -1. `has_chest`, `envs_within` and `town_tier` only answer for `is_land`. Wall and wasteland names are never saved, so the land under them is named once it thaws.
- **`wall_step` is where the next wall stands and where the fallen ones lie, and nothing else** (`WALL_STEP`, or `RING_OF_WALLS_STEP` under the curse, said by the main scene as the world is built and never saved -- to `restore` itself, which draws the rubble as it loads): `_break_wall` and `walls_fallen` read it. **`Encounter.walls_inside` goes on counting in `WALL_STEP`s**, so the land is as hard as it ever was, tile modifiers begin where they always did, and the extra walls are extra gates at the health their own ring gives them.
- **The map only grows when the wall falls** (`_break_wall` -> `_cover`). Arrival grows nothing. **Its environments are generated ahead, on a `WorkerThreadPool` task** (`_generate_ahead`, set off by `walk_onto` a wall tile), into a copy of `_envs` that `_generate` adopts when the area matches -- they are about 95% of the fall's seconds (1.5 s for the first wall, ~5 s for the second). The task touches nothing but its copy, `EnvironmentGenerator`'s statics and the already-built adjacency table; a lost fight leaves it for the next try, which is sound because `_envs` changes nowhere but `_generate`. The dev's skip and the tests' `chart` of a wall without a walk generate in place, as before.
- **The first tile into the fog can be charted blind.** `can_chart` wants generated land or wall beside a charted tile, **seen or not**, and `chart` floors its sight at 0: none at all (the Thick Fog's, no torch held) uncovers the tile taken and nothing round it. With a ring of sight every such tile is already seen, so this is the old rule for everyone else. **Under the Nightwalkers the reach is further** (`dark_reach`, a Callable the main scene points at `Inventory.dark_reach`; `reach()` is 1 without it): any generated land or wall up to that many steps out, across land (the wall only ever ends a way). `can_chart` reads `_chartable`, one walk out from all charted land at once, cleared by `_show`, `_break_wall`, `place_cave` and a changed reach, because the hover asks on every mouse move. `dark_path(cell)` is the way in (fewest tiles, then the start nearest the player), **every tile of which is fought for in turn** -- the main scene's `_dark_way` -- so charting still only ever takes a tile beside the charted land: `walk_onto` refuses a tile further out, and `chart` takes the whole way at once only for the dev's skip. **A lost fight retreats to `chart_from(cell)`.** `HexMap.can_pick` (the builder sets it to `can_chart`) is what lets the hover and a click land on an undrawn cell; its `get_tile_info` is `{}`, so whoever handles the click asks the builder (`env_at`, `level_of`) and **must not ask `name_of`, which would name it**.
- **`mods_of(cell, wild)` is derived, like `has_chest`:** `TileMods.for_cell` off `env_seed` and `ring_of` (not `Encounter.walls_inside`: the count follows the walls that actually stand, Ring of Walls and all), and `[]` for anything but open land -- a settlement, a chest, the wall, the wasteland, and any ring a wall stood on (`on_wall_ring`), fallen or not. `wild` is the Wild Tiles curse, which the builder cannot know.
- **The Gollux cave (`cave`, `place_cave(reach)`, `can_enter_cave`, `_draw_cave`) is one a world and saved** (`MapSave` version 4, `cave` absent for none): placed from `CAVE_FIRST_RING` out to `reach` (the main scene's `Inventory.farthest_land`), never on `on_wall_ring` or a settlement, by `hash([env_seed, "cave"])`, before its land need exist. It is a set piece: `road_at`, `has_chest`, `mods_of`, `can_farm` / `can_farm_ground` (and so `best_farm`) all refuse it, and `name_of` names it from `TileNames.CAVE_FEATURES`. Drawn by `HexMap.set_cave` (`CAVE_ART`, one `CAVE_NODE` on the settlements' layer and anchor) once seen and on land; a missing picture draws nothing.
- **A torch is read at the moment a tile is charted and never again.** `main_scene._sight()` (`BASE_SIGHT` (2) + `inventory.stats().sight`, the base gone under the Thick Fog) is passed to `chart` there only, so putting a torch on uncovers nothing, taking one off hides nothing, and a torch that dropped in that same fight is in the bag and does not count. `_reveal_around` cannot reach past generated land either: `rect` reaches `WASTE_DEPTH` rings past the wall, and no chart is further out than the wall.
- **`level_of(cell)` comes in even bands of `LEVEL_TILES` (4) rings** (level `n` starts at `first_step(n)`), and `START_LAND_RADIUS + 1` and `WALL_STEP` are multiples of it, so the land past a wall is a level above the land inside it; it is the tile panel's number and the ceiling on drops. Enemy health uses the smooth distance instead.
- **`can_farm` asks nothing about where the player stands, and refuses every settlement** (Farm hangs off it); `best_farm()` is the farmable tile farthest out, where the camp stands; `can_move_to` refuses everything while `walking`.
