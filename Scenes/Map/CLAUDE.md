## Map code (`Scenes/Map/`)
A procedurally generated hex map, uncovered tile by tile: a hexagon of land ringed by an ice wall, with frozen wasteland past it. **Read `DESIGN.md` here before changing generation, saving or naming** -- it holds the why.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `hex_grid.gd` (`HexGrid`) | Pointy-top offset grid, odd rows shifted right (same as the TileMapLayers). `Edge` enum (E, SE, SW, W, NW, NE), `EDGES`, `neighbor`, `neighbors`, `opposite`, `distance`, `corners`, `edge_mask` / `mask_edges` |
| `sheet_meta.gd` (`SheetMeta`) | Reads `AI-sprites/spritesheet/hex_tileset.json` once and hands out its `meta` block. `env_adjacency()` has each environment's self-entry stripped |
| `hex_tileset.gd` (`HexTileset`) | Builds the TileSet at runtime from that JSON (56x64 tiles). `atlas_coords`, `road_name`, `blend_name`, `env_rank`, `can_border`, `opaque_mask`, `legal_road_masks`, `env_icon(env)` (the `ENV_ICON` terrain swatch the tile panel and the bounty rows both show) |
| `hex_map.tscn` / `hex_map.gd` (`HexMap`) | GroundLayer, one `Blend_<env>` layer per spreading environment, RoadLayer, Highlight, plus `fog`, `chests` and the `player` token made in code. `set_ground` redraws the blends of the cell **and its six neighbours**, reading undrawn cells through the `hidden_env` Callable. `get_tile_info` (incl. `blends`, `environments`). Signals `tile_hovered`, `tile_clicked`, `dragged`, `cell_aimed`. `aim_radius` (-1 but while land is being chosen: the hover then follows the cursor over undrawn cells, `HexHighlight` outlines every cell within that radius, and a click emits `cell_aimed` instead of selecting). `select_cell`, `deselect`, `selected_cell`, `NO_CELL`, `DRAG_THRESHOLD` |
| `player_token.gd` (`PlayerToken`) | The player's `AnimatedSprite2D` (idle, run), cropped by one shared `BOUNDS`. `walk(path)` (each walk asks the `move_speed` Callable once, so +100% halves `SECONDS_PER_TILE` and doubles the run cycle), `advance(delta)` (tests drive it), `finish_walk()`, signal `arrived`. Dust comes from `_process`, so tests make none |
| `fog_overlay.gd` (`FogOverlay`) | The grey hex over seen-but-uncharted tiles. A removed cell fades over `LIFT_TIME`; `has_cell` is false at once |
| `ambient.gd` (`Ambient`) | Weather particles (`set_env`, from `WEATHER`) and a `CanvasModulate` day cycle. A child of `HexMap`, so it hides with it |
| `chest_pointer.gd` (`ChestPointer`) | A badge pinned to the window's edge toward `target` -- the chest's own sprite on an ink disc ringed with gold, a gold arrow beating towards the chest, drawn at `ui_scale` --, which is only ever a chest a fortuneteller was paid to find; hidden only when the chest is on screen and seen or the map itself is hidden: it stands for as long as the chest does, at `z_index` -1 so every panel on the interface layer draws over it |
| `ice_overlay.gd` (`IceOverlay`) | The stand-in ice (sprite palette's ice ramp) drawn over wall cells (`Kind.WALL`) and seen wasteland (`Kind.WASTE`). `MapBuilder` adds it under `HexMap.chests` after `clear_map` |
| `hex_highlight.gd` | Hover and selection outlines |
| `environment_generator.gd` (`EnvironmentGenerator`) | Random-frontier growth under `allowed()` (the sheet's `env_adjacency`), region size boosts and damping, small-region merge. `extend()` grows into new cells and freezes the old. Tunables at the top |
| `town_world.gd` (`TownWorld`) | 256x256 world of towns laid out **ring by ring round the map's origin** (`generate(seed, origin)`): each ring of land gets `round(spots * TIER_CHANCES)` of each tier (small 1%, medium 0.5%, fortress 0.1%), **the fortress floored at one**, none within `KEEP_OUT` of the origin, never adjacent; each linked to its 1/2/4 nearest within 20 steps. `ring_of(steps)`. `to_dict` / `from_dict` for the save |
| `road_network.gd` (`RoadNetwork`) | A* roads along town links, only in shapes the sprites have; roads stop at town edges. `extend()` adds links a bigger window reaches and never moves a laid road |
| `map_builder.gd` (`MapBuilder`) | Generates and draws the map. The land is the hexagon within `land_radius` (`START_LAND_RADIUS` 10, saved) of cell (0, 0); ring `land_radius + 1` is the ice wall (`is_wall`), everything past it wasteland (`is_wasteland`), `is_land` the rest. `rect` covers `WASTE_DEPTH` rings past the wall (`_cover`). Charting a wall tile (`_break_wall`) moves `land_radius` out by `WALL_STEP` and thaws seen wasteland inside it. `migrated_radius(states)` places the wall for a pre-wall save. `walls_fallen()` is how many have come down in this world (heirloom picks, and the fortuneteller's way out). Cell `State`: `HIDDEN`, `UNCHARTED`, `CHARTED`. `create` / `restore` / `to_save`; `chart(cell, sight := 1)` (the fog comes off every hidden tile within `sight` steps of the charted one, through the shared `_reveal_around`, which `scour` also uses), `can_chart`, `chart_from`, `walk_onto` (to `chart_from`, then the step onto the uncharted tile, where `player_cell` stands until the fight is decided; `chart` of the tile underfoot arrives in place), `move_to`, `can_move_to`, `route_to`, `can_farm`, `town_tier`, `can_visit` (charted, standing on it, not walking), `homes()` / `jump_to(cell)` (the fortuneteller's homecoming: every charted settlement but the one underfoot, and the player put down on one with no walk at all -- `_on_player_arrived` runs, so an arrival's every consequence follows), `walking`, signal `arrived`; `name_of`, `level_of`, `circle_of` (1 inside the first wall, +1 a `WALL_STEP` of distance, the wall ring counting with the land it opens; what a drop may be made of), `area_variant`, `seen`, `has_chest`, `nearest_chest`, `scour(center)` (the fortuneteller's: hidden tiles round `center` come out UNCHARTED, seen ones are left alone, returns how many), `envs_within(cell, steps)` (what a bounty board may post from), `nearest_env(envs, min_level)` (the closest **seen** tile of that land to the player, charted first, never a settlement, none shallower than `min_level`), `reveal_all`. `origin` is the world spot at cell (0, 0); `_spot(cell)` crosses from cells to world spots |
| `tile_names.gd` (`TileNames`) | `generate`: a made-up word plus a feature word from the environment or the settlement tier. A pure function of map seed and cell; names neither `MapBuilder` nor `TownWorld` |
| `map_save.gd` (`MapSave`) | `user://map.json`: seeds, origin, window, the whole town world, land, roads, tile names, states, player cell. Env and state rows are one string per map row against a legend. `fingerprint(tileset)` hashes the three sheet tables a map is read through. `save` goes through `SafeFile`; `load_from(path, problem, expect_sheet)` |

## Rules and gotchas
- **Generation rules come from the sheet's JSON meta** (`blend_rule`, `blend_priority`, `env_adjacency`) through `SheetMeta`: `HexTileset.can_border` and `EnvironmentGenerator.allowed()` are one table, so the generator cannot make a border the sprites cannot draw.
- **`TownWorld.RING` and `KEEP_OUT` are `MapBuilder.WALL_STEP` / `START_LAND_RADIUS` and `START_TOWN_DISTANCE`, held together by `test_generation`:** TownWorld may not name MapBuilder, which names it. Rings are counted in `WALL_STEP`s whatever the Ring of Walls says, like `Encounter.walls_inside`, so under that curse two of its five-wide rings share one fortress.
- **Map origins must be on even rows** (`MapBuilder` asserts it), or world neighbours stop matching on-screen ones.
- **Nothing generated is ever regenerated.** Both generators are order-dependent, not seed-dependent, so the same seed and rect do *not* give the same land. Land, roads, the town world and tile names are all written into the save; growing the map only ever adds.
- **A tile is named once, when it first comes out of the fog (`_show`), and the name is saved.** Do not recompute names on load.
- **`MapSave.load_from` has two nulls:** empty `problem` is a first run; a reason in `problem` is a refusal, and the caller must stop and never write over the file.
- **`MapSave` never names `MapBuilder`** (Godot's cycle checker): states are raw ints there, with `STATE_NAMES` as the legend.
- **Drawing a whole map goes through `_draw_saved`, not `_show`:** `set_ground` refreshes seven cells per call.
- **`MapBuilder.create` clears towns within `START_TOWN_DISTANCE` (5) of the centre and guarantees a small one exactly that far out,** linked by road to cell (0, 0). `restore` does none of that: the saved world already has it.
- **Chests are derived, not saved:** `has_chest` is a per-cell roll (open land, `CHEST_MIN_DISTANCE` out) that holds until the tile is charted. A chest is drawn once seen, or anywhere under the dev's `Settings.show_all_chests()` (`redraw_chests` re-asks every cell). `nearest_chest()` scans every cell, so call it on arrival only, never per frame.
- **`nearest_env` never points at a hidden cell** -- naming land the player has not found is giving away what charting is for -- and prefers a **charted** tile to a seen one at the same distance, because a charted tile can be farmed. It measures from `player_cell` and **never offers a settlement**, so a board cannot answer "where does it live" with the town the player is standing in. It and `envs_within` scan like `nearest_chest`: ask them when a page is drawn or a town is walked into.
- **The fog gives way to gold in two places and nowhere else:** `scour` and the chest star, both the fortuneteller's. `nearest_env` still never names hidden land, paid or not.
- **Nothing past the wall is the player's until it falls.** Wasteland is seen as snow (no ground, no road, no fog veil, `name_of` is `WASTE_NAME`), never charted or walked to. A wall tile draws its real ground under the ice but reads as `WALL_NAME`, `area_variant` "plain" and `town_tier` -1. `has_chest`, `envs_within`, `nearest_env` and `town_tier` only answer for `is_land`. Wall and wasteland names are never saved, so the land under them is named once it thaws.
- **`wall_step` is where the next wall stands and nothing else** (`WALL_STEP`, or `RING_OF_WALLS_STEP` under the curse, said by the main scene as the world is built and never saved): `_break_wall` and `walls_fallen` read it. **`Encounter.walls_inside` goes on counting in `WALL_STEP`s**, so the land is as hard as it ever was, tile modifiers begin where they always did, and the extra walls are extra gates at the health their own ring gives them.
- **The map only grows when the wall falls** (`_break_wall` -> `_cover`), about 1 s for the ring-21 hexagon. Arrival grows nothing.
- **The first tile into the fog can be charted blind.** `can_chart` wants generated land or wall beside a charted tile, **seen or not**, and `chart` floors its sight at 0: none at all (the Thick Fog's, no torch held) uncovers the tile taken and nothing round it. With a ring of sight every such tile is already seen, so this is the old rule for everyone else. `HexMap.can_pick` (the builder sets it to `can_chart`) is what lets the hover and a click land on an undrawn cell; its `get_tile_info` is `{}`, so whoever handles the click asks the builder (`env_at`, `level_of`) and **must not ask `name_of`, which would name it**.
- **`mods_of(cell, wild)` is derived, like `has_chest`:** `TileMods.for_cell` off `env_seed` and `ring_of` (not `Encounter.walls_inside`: the count follows the walls that actually stand, Ring of Walls and all), and `[]` for anything but open land -- a settlement, a chest, the wall, the wasteland, and any ring a wall stood on (`on_wall_ring`), fallen or not. `wild` is the Wild Tiles curse, which the builder cannot know.
- **A torch is read at the moment a tile is charted and never again.** `main_scene._sight()` (`1 + inventory.stats().sight`, the 1 gone under the Thick Fog) is passed to `chart` there and in the dev skip-fight only, so putting a torch on uncovers nothing, taking one off hides nothing, and a torch that dropped in that same fight is in the bag and does not count. `_reveal_around` cannot reach past generated land either: `rect` reaches `WASTE_DEPTH` rings past the wall, and no chart is further out than the wall.
- **`level_of(cell)` comes in widening bands** (level `n` starts at the nth triangular number of steps); it is the tile panel's number and the ceiling on drops. Enemy health uses the smooth distance instead.
- **`can_farm` asks nothing about where the player stands, and refuses every settlement** (Farm hangs off it); `best_farm()` is the farmable tile farthest out, where the camp stands; `can_move_to` refuses everything while `walking`.
