## Items (`Scenes/Items/`)
Every kill rolls for gear (about one body in thirty), always hands over gold, and rolls separately for an orb. A body that does leave gear rolls again for more, up to `Encounter.MOST_DROPS`. Every drop is its own `Item` with a rarity, a level and modifiers; nothing stacks. **Read `DESIGN.md` here before changing what stats, modifiers, rarities or orbs mean** -- it holds the why.

## Rules and gotchas
- **The Broken Torch is the Broken Sword's twin** (`LootTable.BROKEN_TORCH`, kind `broken_torch`): weight 0 so no roll deals one, a Wooden Torch's Sight and the torch's affixes, with its own picture (pixellab, 2026-09-24). `Inventory.transcended()` puts one in the new bag under the Thick Fog and nothing else hands one out.
- **A kind can begin past the plainest material** (the greaves, at iron: `tier_levels` `[3, 5, 6, 7]`). Read a piece's strength step from its `material`, never its `tier` (its place in the kind's list); under `first_level` a roll is dealt as the slot's plainest piece, and a bounty promising the kind lifts the piece to `first_level`.
- **`Item.ascend()` is one plus more** -- every modifier but a held-fast one kept in its place as the band moves up `PLUS_LEVELS` -- and is both the Orb of Ascension's act and what Lean Pickings does to a find as it falls, so a +n piece can now be a drop and not only an heirloom.
- **A piece past item level `LootTable.NEEDS_FROM` (5) asks for its kind's attribute** (`"needs"` in `KINDS`; the jewellery asks none): `requirement(type, level)` is `scale(attribute, NEEDS_LINE, level)`, about one middling line of its level. **It gates Equip and nothing else** (`Inventory.why_not_equip`, "Needs 28 Strength"): a worn piece keeps working when the points later fall short. The points are `points_without(displaced)` -- `attributes()` over both dolls with what the swap takes off removed, so the piece never lifts itself; the heirlooms reach the player's through `keeper`, a `WeakRef` `stash()` sets. `ItemDetails` writes "Requires n Strength" under the rarity line. A test fixture wearing a deep piece for another rule puts it on through `Equipment.equip`.
- **`Inventory.curses` is the world's and `pending_curses` is only chosen:** `effects()` tells the fight the first as `Curses.effect(id)`, `stats()` adds each row's `stats`, `credit_walls` pays two a wall under the Long Winter, and `transcended()` is the one place the second becomes the first (the old world's end with it). Saved by name since version 18; an older save is a world under none. **`skill_worth()` is what a skill point counts for -- 2 under Hard Lessons:** `stats()` scales the skills' flat and percent sums by it (`_skills_worth`, a copy; the capstones' effects are not numbers and are not doubled) and `SkillCard.fill` describes that many points, so the card and the fight agree.
- **Only Gollux pays skulls** (the user's, 2026-10-03): `skull_allowance()` is `dungeon_depth` -- exactly one skull a depth won -- and nothing else -- no wall, no curse carried -- what `TranscendPage` may spend, whole again in every world, a lost one too. The `skull_budget` a version 21-31 save carries is ignored.
- **`dungeon_floors` (version 26) is the leaderboard's score -- every floor ever beaten in one descent, from the top -- and is the player's like `dungeon_depth`:** carried by `transcended()`, only ever raised (by the main scene's `_bank_depths`), and a version 25 save reads as `dungeon_depth * Encounter.DUNGEON.enemies`.
- **`farthest_land` (version 25) is the player's, like `dungeon_depth`:** carried by `transcended()` (the `seeing_stone` saved beside it until 2026-10-03 is ignored). `reach(radius)` only raises the first (the main scene hands it `view.land_radius` in `_credit_walls`, which is also how a version 24 save learns it), and it bounds where every world's Gollux cave may go (`MapBuilder.place_cave`).
- **What the second batch of curses asks of the inventory:** `skill_worth()` is a float and its shares add (Hard Lessons +1, Specialist +0.5); **`why_not_skill(id)` / `rank_up_skill(id)` are what the skills page asks, never `skills`**, because the Specialist's one tree is the inventory's to know. `collection_bonus()` is nothing under Forgotten, and `uniques_doubled` (saved, carried over, only grows) counts again ever after. `why_not_equip(item, socket)` is the one reason Equip is dead, and `can_equip` is it, empty; **`most_worn` is set on the heirlooms by `stash()` and never saved**, which is how the Lone Heir reaches a second `Inventory` that has no curses -- `transcended()` bares that doll as the cursed world begins, and `make_heirloom` ascends the piece made at its end. `credit_walls` pays `1 + Long Winter + No Second Chances` a wall. `homeland` (saved) is the Homeland's two lands, empty until the main scene settles it, and rides in `effects()` as `Curses.HOME_PREFIX + env`. Save version 20.
- **An item's level and base stats are frozen when it is rolled** and saved with it; an orb changes only its rarity and modifiers. **The one exception is the fortress smith** (`Scenes/Town/blacksmith.gd`): an upgrade raises the level by one and rewrites `stats` to `Item.scaled_stats` at the new level, and leaves every modifier's tier and number as they were (`Item.level_up`) -- except a perfected line, which goes to the new top, and **an heirloom's line, which climbs back towards its `"peak"`**: tier = the larger of where it is and `min(peak, mod_level)`, the number keeping its place in the band. Nothing else may move the level or the base stats. An `Item` built by hand (tests do this) must be given `stats` or it is worth nothing.
- **Crafting happens on a piece the bag page has open, carried or worn, and never on one off a shelf.** The orb tray crafts on a doll square and the smith works on a worn piece the same way: no fight can be on while those pages are up, so nothing else is holding the `Item`.
- **A broken piece is out of the game as far as changing it goes** -- no orb, no upgrade, no lock -- and a **locked** modifier (`{"id", "value", "locked": true}`) survives every orb: `_reroll_at` keeps it and fills the rest around it, and Divine skips its value. **A held-fast line (locked or bound) is frozen in number and band:** `Item.hold(index, flag)` sets the flag and `"at"`, the level its band is read at (`band_level(mod)`, which detailed lines and `refresh_perfect` read); `ascend()` and the smith's upgrade walk past it, and only `transcend()` moves it -- rescaled from its own band to the new level's, `"at"` moved with it. A line locked before `"at"` existed reads its band at the piece's level. No orb makes a common (Transmutation's uncommon is as low as one goes, and its two hold both held-fast lines), so a locked piece is never a common (a contradiction in `ItemRarity.MOD_COUNT`). A lock is one of the rarity's handful and never an extra on top of it.
- **`LootTable.scale` is the only place that knows what a level does.** PERCENT and GLOBAL mods take the multiplier only, `CHANCE_STATS` (crit, drop rate, gold find, item rarity, bleed, sight, spawn speed, fight clock) take the flat step only, and a FLAT mod may name its own step (`level_flat`).
- **A kind's power and a material's are applied after `scale`, by `Item.scaled_stats` alone** (`LootTable.power_of`), so the smith's upgrade follows for free. Never write a kind's factor into `stats`: `LEVEL_FLAT` adds the same damage a level (0.4) to every weapon, so a dagger written as `damage: 0.6` would be a sword again by level 10. The material's share skips `CHANCE_STATS` and `RATE_STATS`; the kind's does not, because no kind names a factor for a chance.
- **A material is gated by the tile's level, never the piece's own** (the user's, 2026-09-28). `roll` hands its `tile_level` to `_weighted(rng, level)`, which draws the kind by weight (`roll_kind`, which a bounty's promise draws on its own) and then `_tier_at` draws **evenly among every material that level has unlocked** -- no draw at all while only one is, so the shallow game costs exactly the rolls it always did. Kind weights sum to 252 (21 x 12), which is what keeps each **slot's** share of drops what it was. The vendor's shelf and a bounty's promised piece pass the town's tile level the same way. `TIER_MIN_LEVEL` is `[1, 3, 5, 7, 9]` -- a material every two tile levels: 1-2 wood, 3-4 wood and iron, 5-6 up to steel -- the greaves' `[3, 5, 7, 9]`, the torch's `[1, 5, 9]`: the fifth material, **Masterwork <kind>**, from a level-9 tile (the third circle), on every kind with materials but the jewellery and the two broken pieces. The masterwork torch is 3 Sight.
- **The eight pieces the game shipped with are tier 0 of their kind and keep the numbers they always had** -- the Wooden Torch aside, whose base is Sight alone now. Their kind is written first for its slot (the offhand's first is the shield, the torch being the other shipped piece in it), so anything asking for "a plain piece for this socket" still picks up the wooden one, and old saves, the uniques' bases and `icon_path`'s fallback all land where they always did. Pieces already held are frozen either way.
- **A tile's level is a ceiling, not a payout:** a drop rolls evenly up to `level_of(cell) + TIER_LEVEL[tier]`, with rarity lifting the floor. Balance numbers describe a piece at the ceiling.
- **`stats`, `affixes` and `globals` must not overlap,** and every affix needs a FLAT mod naming it and every global a GLOBAL one, or it is unreachable. `test_inventory._test_items` fails on all of it. `_test_slot_locks` pins which stats are locked to which piece.
- **A GLOBAL mod is applied by `Equipment.totals`, never by `Item.effective_stats`,** and two of them add rather than compound.
- **Increased item rarity lifts weights, it does not add steps;** UNIQUE has no weight in `TIER_WEIGHTS`, so it never comes out of `ItemRarity.roll`. A test holds that. **Uniques are rolled by `UniqueTable.roll` instead,** beside the gear, the way orbs are -- and item rarity reaches them there: `chance_for` multiplies by `1 + rarity% x RARITY_STEP[UNIQUE]` (1, as strong as the drop rate), after the drop rate (2026-09-28, the user's ask; 4 was tried first and taken out).
- **A unique is an `Item` with `unique` set:** `type` is still its base piece (slot, base stats, the smith's upgrade), its rarity is UNIQUE, and its modifiers are its row's -- always those, in that order, only the values rolled (`Item.rolled_unique`). It may carry a line its base could never roll; `effective_stats` applies by kind and never asks the pool. **Only Orbs of Divine and Chaos apply to one** (`OrbTable.can_apply`, beside the `broken` line). A retired id loads as null, like a retired piece.
- **`ModifierTable.UNIQUE_ONLY` names the modifiers only a unique's row may carry, and is empty today:** `added_gold_find` was the one, and the Gold Ring shows gold find as a base stat now, so `can_roll` lets it into that pool. The list stays for the next row that wants it, and `test_inventory` tells one from a dead modifier by it, as it does `DORMANT`. No row may carry a PERCENT and a GLOBAL on one stat: they write the same sentence.
- **`spawn_speed` rolls on every piece** (`LootTable.ANY_AFFIXES`, read by `can_roll` beside the kind's own `affixes`), is a `CHANCE_STAT` with a flat step of 0 and one band at every level (`added_spawn_speed` 5-15, `level_flat` 0): the 100% cap is a set's worth of top rolls, not a level's.
- **`gold_find` and `item_rarity` are in `CHANCE_STATS`** for `drop_rate`'s reason -- each multiplies something already exponential -- so a level adds a point and never multiplies. `bleed` is there because a share of a hit that compounds is a share past everything -- 20% of a blow would be 530% of it by level 30 -- and `sight` so that a level cannot multiply one tile into twenty-six; its flat step is 0 as well, the material being the only thing that moves it.
- **The four finders are a broad one and three narrow ones, so the narrow ones carry the bigger numbers.** `drop_rate` lifts gear, uniques, orbs and gold alike (5% on the Gold Amulet, `added_drop_rate` 3-10); `item_rarity` lifts the weights a drop rolls its rarity on and the unique chance (15% on the Opal Ring, `added_item_rarity` 10-25, on all eight jewels); `gold_find` only the purse (20% on the Gold Ring, `added_gold_find` with `level_flat` 0, which is what keeps The Tithe's band where it was); `orb_find` only the orb chance (15% on the Pearl Ring, no modifier). All four are `CHANCE_STATS` taking a point a level.
- **`Equipment.effects()` is one `UniqueTable.effect_of` a worn unique:** a home piece is `"home:<env>"`, its damage on that ground alone. The Pilgrim's set, and the home pieces' second rules with it, went on 2026-09-29 (the user's, "for now").
- **Two dolls are worn at once, the ordinary one and the heirlooms': flats add, percents multiply.** `Inventory.stats()` hands `stash().equipment` to `Equipment.totals` as `also`. **A stat is global** (the Spiked Helm and Heartwood read the armour of all sixteen, Bulwark the block) **and so is anything that shapes the fight; a count and a set are per side:** the Ascetic's Cord counts the bare places on the doll it is on, the Packmule's Harness the loose pieces beside its doll (`Inventory._side_count`; one on each doll counts both). The Rag and Bone Sack on either doll pays for everything thrown away, heirlooms included. **The attributes are the one count that is global** (the user's ruling, 2026-09-27): `Inventory.attributes()` adds them over both dolls, and the Patchwork Coat's attribute lines and the Purist's Seal's pieces without one are counted over all sixteen. **When a new unique could be read either way, ask the user; never guess.**
- **An heirloom is a piece in `stash()`, and nothing on the `Item` says so** but `safe_level`, which only the end of a world sets. It is made only on `TranscendPage`, one a transcension. It never goes back to the bag, is never sold, and can be discarded one at a time. **`transcended()` works on copies,** so a caller whose write fails is still holding the heirlooms as they were. What survives it: the stash and its doll, unspent super orbs, `uniques_found`, `kills`, `tips`, and `first_sword_taken` forced true -- which with the kills is what keeps a second world from dealing the first one's helping hands.
- **What a super orb leaves on a piece every other hand honours.** `OrbTable._reroll_at` keeps every `held_fast` modifier and counts `extra_slot` into the handful (`_room` too); `reroll_values` -- Divine's -- steps over `held_fast` and `"perfect"` alike; the smith's upgrade and `transcend()` call `refresh_perfect()`. **Anything that rolls a new line for a piece already made asks `item.mod_level()`, never `item.level`, and anything that rerolls or writes the band of a line it has asks `item.tier_of(mod)`.** A Divine and the smith keep the tier (the smith lifts only an heirloom's, towards its `"peak"`); Perfection is the top tier's top (`refresh_perfect` erases `"under"`).** Replacement writes a fresh dictionary, so a perfected line replaced is perfect no longer. A unique refuses Replacement, Expansion and Binding (its lines are its row's); a broken piece takes Mending and nothing else.
- **`Inventory.stats()` carries what a unique asks of the player:** the Spiked Helm's share of the set's armour goes in with the skills' flat damage (so the percents scale it), and `bare_sockets` / `bag_pieces` ride along for the Ascetic's Cord and the Packmule's Harness. **The attributes go through `attributes()` first** -- both dolls, the Heirloom's Echo, the Crown of Accord, then the Zealot's Brand -- and everything reads those counted points: the stats' own `strength` / `dexterity` / `intelligence`, their shares (`attribute_gift`, which the character page asks; intelligence gives damage under the Scholar's Circlet), the flats the Ogre's Knuckle, Fencer's Signet, Butcher's Cleaver and Quickdraw Boots add beside the skills', and `skill_worth()` under the Sage's Abacus. `attribute_lines` / `pure_pieces` ride along for the Patchwork Coat and the Purist's Seal. `Inventory.salvage(item)` is `TownPrices.salvage_price(item, share)` at the Sack's rank's share while it is worn, else 0.
- **Found is found:** `uniques_found` only grows, and `FightLedger.add_loot` is the one place a find writes it, as the find lands. **Each one found is `UniqueTable.COLLECTION_DAMAGE` (1) percent more damage, worn or not** (`Inventory.collection_bonus`), applied in `Inventory.stats()` as its own multiplier after gear and skills -- never in `Equipment.totals`, which knows nothing of the log.
- **Defence is four stats, all spent on the fight clock** (`Encounter.taken`): `armor` and `dodge` are ratings that grow with the level like any quantity (neither is a chance on the gear -- the fight turns them into a share of each blow), and `block` and `time_on_hit` are **tenths of a second** (`SECONDS_STATS`), so a whole-number roll can be a fraction of one. Block lives only on what is held; the helmets and body armour split into armour (helm, plate) and dodge (hood, jerkin). Health, energy shield, regen, leech and the resistances are gone and load as nothing (`Item.from_dict` drops a stat `STAT_LABELS` has not got), and the hat, robe, slippers and Sapphire Amulet with them (a retired kind loads as null).
- **Nothing destroys gear to fit the bag.** A drop, a run's bank or a save over `CAPACITY` stays whole and makes the player `encumbered()`; `main_scene` greys Chart / Farm and halves the walk until the bag is back at the cap. `order()` is level-major; `_test_bag_order` pins it.
- **A piece is in the bag or a socket, never both:** always go through `Inventory.equip` / `unequip`. Both refuse on a full bag: `unequip` always grows it, and `equip` grows it too when a two-hander takes the offhand off with the weapon (`can_equip`, the arithmetic `BagPage` greys its Equip by). Worn gear is outside `CAPACITY`.
- **A two-hander closes the offhand** (`LootTable.two_handed`, the greatswords'): `Equipment.displaced_by` is the one answer to what a swap costs, so the button's tooltip, the comparison's columns and what `equip` actually takes off cannot disagree, and a save holding an offhand beside a two-hander loses the offhand. It costs the torch too, so a greatsword charts one ring of map less.
- **Gold and orbs are not in `items`:** never capped, sorted or autodiscarded. A spent-out orb is erased, not left at zero.
- **Every modifier value is a whole number,** so saves hold `14` not `14.0` and round-tripped items compare with `==`. Stats are stored as the number they display.
- **Names, not enum positions, go on disk** (rarities, sockets, orbs). A retired item, mod, orb or ill-fitting socket is dropped on load rather than guessed at.
- **`Inventory` never saves itself** and the path is always a parameter, so tests never touch the player's save. A file that is there and cannot be honoured comes back empty **with a reason in `problem`**, and the caller must then never save over it.
- **`ItemRarity` must never mention `Item`,** and a `const` must not name another class's enum: that is where Godot's cycle checker bites.
- **A unique drops only once it is unlocked** (`Achievements.unlocked`: the starters and every earned achievement's unique), and still only by luck after `UniqueTable.FIRST_UNIQUE_KILLS`. The fight is handed the list (`Encounter.unlocked`), the bounty board's promised unique draws from it (`BountyBoard.reward_item(.., unlocked)`), and the collection log draws a locked one black. **Found is not unlocked:** an old save's finds stay in the log and must be earned again to drop (the user's ruling). **`achievements` (id -> rank since version 29; a version 28 list is rank I), `achievements_new` and `tally` are the player's** (version 28): saved, carried by `transcended()` (which ticks `transcended`), never reset. `Inventory.tick(key, n)` is the one way a count is added outside a fight (`discarded` from all four discard paths, `chaos` from the bag's `_craft`, `walls` from `credit_walls`, every wall ever broken), and `Inventory.save_written`, said at the end of every `save`, is where the main scene checks them (`_check_achievements`, deferred). `Inventory` already had a `count(type)`, hence `tick`.
- **The starters' numbers are `Inventory`'s** (`SQUIRE_DAMAGE` and `COURIER_SPAWN` as flats in `stats()`' `turned`, `NOVICE_XP` added to `xp_more` while `level < NOVICE_UNTIL`) or the fight's (`Encounter.BEGINNERS_LUCK`, the Worry Stone's save). **The Wayfarer's Torch is the one unique with no rule** (the user's stat stick): `effect` and `effect_text` are empty, `ItemDetails` skips the empty line, and `test_inventory` allows it for that id alone.
- **A rank is the player's and read live, never written onto an item:** every number a ranked unique's rule reads is `UniqueTable.dial(id, key, rank)` -- `Inventory._dial` at its own rank (`Achievements.rank`, rank I for one worn and not earned), `Encounter._dial` at the rank `wear` was told, a card at `UniqueTable.ranks` -- so the card and the rule are one figure and every copy held grows at once. A rank IV line asks `_peak(id)` beside whether the piece is worn, its numbers are plain consts beside the rule it grows (`Inventory.ACCORD_WITHIN_PEAK` ... `PURIST_MODS`), and the card writes it only once it is reached (`Item.peak_text`, the user's call). **Anything that counts gear as it is not works on copies** (`_counted_dolls`), so being counted never changes what the player holds.
