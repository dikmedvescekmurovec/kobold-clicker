## Combat code (`Scenes/Combat/`)
A tile is taken by beating a lineup of enemies against a clock; a taken tile can be farmed endlessly with no clock, or **camped** -- that same run with nobody clicking, fought while the game is shut. **Read `DESIGN.md` here before changing the HUD's layout, what drops show as, or the fight profiles** -- it holds the why.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `encounter.gd` (`Encounter`) | The rules, no nodes. `for_tile(cell, env, variant, chest)` rolls a lineup seeded from the cell; `farm(...)` is the same fight `endless`. `PROFILES` (keyed by `MapBuilder.area_variant`): `ORDINARY` is ten enemies in 30 s ending on an elite, `SETTLEMENT` fifteen in 60 s with an elite every fifth and a boss last, `CHEST` a lone `MIMIC`. `for_wall(cell)` is the ice wall: `WALL` profile, the lone `WALL_NAME` in 60 s on the `ice` backdrop, health `hp_of * WALL_HP` and no growth term of its own -- `hp_of` already steps once per wall, and a wall's ring counts none of itself, so the wall on ring 21 is `WALL_GROWTH` times the one on 11 on top of the walk between them, about fifteen times over (`WALL_HP` is the dial for how hard a wall is; `test_combat._test_the_ice_wall` pins bare hands and bare farmed rares out, and farmed rares with the whole Power tree at 1-8 clicks/s; `tests/balance_wall.gd` plays it out in kills). `arm(stats)` reads the player's stats once; `hit()` is a click, `advance(delta)` runs the clock, auto-swings, lets the enemy strike (`_be_struck`) and then wears the body down (`_wear_down`, Heatstroke and bleed), `stop()` ends a run, `kills()` is its score. **Strikes:** while `strikes` (the main scene's, off in a hand-built fight) a standing enemy hits every `ATTACK_EVERY[tier]` (common 1 s, elite 2, boss 4) for `hit_of(enemy, cell)` = `HIT_SECONDS * base_hp(cell) / BASE_HP * HIT_TIER[tier]` seconds of clock; `_struck_by(hit)` rolls `dodge_chance()`, else takes `taken(hit)` = the hit less armour's share (`rating / (rating + ARMOUR_K)`, the same share of any blow) less block, adds it to `wounds`, and emits `player_hit(taken, dodged, blocked)`. A landed hit of the player's gives back `min(time_on_hit, wounds)`. Signals per enemy, hit, death and drop (`loot_dropped`, `gold_dropped`, `orb_dropped`, `xp_dropped`), then `won` / `lost`. `tier_in(slot)` is the tier both `KillPips` and `HealthBar` read. Health is `BASE_HP * HP_GROWTH ^ steps * WALL_GROWTH ^ walls_inside(cell) * hp_modifier` -- **every wall behind a cell multiplies its bodies by `WALL_GROWTH`**, which is what keeps the land a fallen wall opens from dying to one click; gold is `base_gold(cell) * hp_modifier` and does **not** step with it, and `base_gold` is `gold_at_steps(distance)` so a price can be quoted in bodies with no cell to point at (`TownPrices`) |
| `tile_mods.gd` (`TileMods`) | What land past the second wall does to its own fight, and what it pays. `MODS`: id -> `name`, `text`, `reward`, `weight`, `farm`, `not_with`, and the dials `Encounter` reads by key -- `enemies` / `seconds` (added), `elite_every` (put in place), `hp` / `hit` / `attack` (shares, added), `armor` / `dodge` / `block` / `time_on_hit` / `swing` / `walk_in` (factors), and the pay `drop_rate` / `item_rarity` / `gold_find` / `xp`. `for_cell(map_seed, cell, walls, wild)` is a pure function (none short of `FROM_WALLS` 2, then 1, 1-2, 2-3; `wild` a wall sooner and one more), `farmable`, `total`, `factor`. `MapBuilder.mods_of` is its one caller in the game |
| `curses.gd` (`Curses`) | The eleven world curses as a table: id -> `name`, `skulls`, `text`, `reward`, and `stats` (what `Inventory.stats()` adds; `xp_more` is read by `Encounter.arm` alone). `MOST` 3, `effect(id)` = `"curse:<id>"`, `known(saved)` drops ids this build has not got |
| `fight_ledger.gd` (`FightLedger`) | What one fight has earned and the one bank-or-pouch rule (see `Scenes/CLAUDE.md`). No nodes; the main scene makes one per fight. `add_loot/gold/orb/xp`, `add_kill(enemy)` (a body for the bounty boards, `BountyBoard.count_kill`: counted and saved at once for a tile fight, pouched in `slain` and applied by `bank()` for a run), `autodiscarded`, `discard`, `room_left()`, `pending_xp()`, `bank()` (safe twice), `bank_kills` |
| `drops_view.gd` (`DropsView`) | A fight's finds as a grid of `ItemSlot`s on the bag's light `TextPanel`, with the orbs it is handed in a row under them, one find openable into an `ItemDetails` block; used by both the verdict panel and the loot popup. With `discardable`, emits `discarded(item)` and never edits its own list. `resized_contents` tells the owner to re-centre |
| `camp.gd` (`Camp`) | Resting on a taken tile, all rules and no nodes. A camp is the tile's farm run with nobody clicking: `rates(fight)` plays an armed endless `Encounter` for a sample (`SAMPLE_SECONDS` 120, on to `SAMPLE_KILLS` 5 bodies, `SAMPLE_MOST` 1800 either way, at `SAMPLE_STEP`) and hands back what it earned a second in gold, experience and bodies. `make(cell, place, fight, at)` is the drawer saved in `inventory.camp` -- `SINCE`, `CELL`, `PLACE` and those three rates -- and from then on a camp is arithmetic: `earned(camp, at)` is rate times seconds, clamped to `MAX_SECONDS` (12 h) and never negative, with `full` saying the cap is what stopped it. `hunts(fight)` is whether anything would swing at all (`_swing_weapon`'s own two conditions). `cell_of`, `spell_time` |
| `camp_scene.gd` (`CampScene`) | The camp on screen: the tile's own backdrop at dusk (`NIGHT`) with **nobody on it**, and a centred panel -- the place, what a camp is and is not, then Resting / Driven off / Gold / Experience refreshed once a second off `Camp.earned`, the cap's sentence once it bites, and **Break camp**. `begin(camp, env, variant, ui_scale)`, signal `broke_camp`. A `CanvasLayer` on the fight's own layer 2, for `CombatScene`'s reason |
| `combat_actor.gd` (`CombatActor`) | An `AnimatedSprite2D` built from a sheet at runtime, for both sides. Scaled and stood up by its idle frames, so `position` is the middle of its feet |
| `combat_scene.tscn` / `combat_scene.gd` (`CombatScene`) | The view, a `CanvasLayer` (2) the main scene adds over the hidden map: `begin(fight, cell, ui_scale, variant)`. Top centre: place name and level, `KillPips`, clock. Bottom centre: enemy name and `HealthBar`. Bottom-right: loot counter (opens `_loot_panel`). Top-right: Terminate (a run: `stop()`; a tile fight: `give_up()`, a loss whose verdict says "Given up"). Signals out: `finished(won)`, `retry`, `loot_kept`, `loot_discarded`, `drop_discarded`, `gold_gained`, `orb_gained`, `xp_gained`, `xp_absorbed`. Set from outside: `autodiscard` (Callable), `bag_room` (-1 = unknown), `place`, `xp_target`. `hud_bottom()` is the bottom of the top-centre column, for anything the main scene stands over a fight and has to keep off it. Backdrop is `Assets/Area/<env>_<variant>_<n>.png` via `backdrop_for` / `layout_for(cell)` |

`EnemyRoster` is in `Scenes/Enemies/`. Effects (`burst`, `hit_stop`, `shake`) come from `Juice` in `Scenes/UI/`.

## Rules and gotchas
- **A tile's modifiers reach a fight through `for_tile(.., mods)` / `farm(.., mods)` and nowhere else,** and a fight nobody tells has none. `_take_mods` shapes the fight right after the profile and before the lineup is rolled; what they do to the player's numbers and what they pay is the tail of `arm()`, added to the finders so `_kill` has no second path. **`farm` keeps only `TileMods.farmable`:** a run has no clock and is never struck, so a clock or strike modifier there would be pay for nothing. A chest and the wall carry none. Barren and Gilded are asked for by id in `_kill`.
- **A curse is one `if` on `Curses.effect(id) in effects`, like a worn unique's,** and the ones that shape the fight are taken on once in `wear` (`_take_curses`, `_cursed`): Iron Foes and the Bloodthirst add to the tile's own shares, Short Days cuts every clock with one, and a body's health is sized again through `_health_of` -- **only under a curse that moves health, so a fight built by hand keeps the health it was given**. `_health_of` is the one place a body's starting health is worked out; the ice wall answers there to `WALL_HP` and the Long Winter and to nothing else.
- **A finished chance is halved or doubled through `_lifted(rate, factor)`,** never by adding points: Lean Pickings hands every gear roll `_gear_rate()` (the purse, the orbs and the unique roll keep the full `drop_rate`), the Thick Fog hands the unique roll twice its chance. `_lean(item)` is the curse's pay -- a find falls +1 or +2 (`LEAN_PLUS`, `Item.ascend`) -- drawn on `loot_rng` **only under the curse**, so nobody else's loot stream moves; never the Broken Sword. **Hard Lessons is a "less" too:** `_kill` multiplies the finished experience by `LESSONS_XP` after every `xp_more` has been added, so a camp, which samples a real run, is taught as slowly.
- **`walk_in` is the fight's own** (`WALK_IN`, doubled in a Mire): the view divides by `fight.walk_in`, tests that play an unmodified fight still step by the const.
- **A camp pays gold and experience and nothing else:** no gear, no orbs, no bodies for a bounty board and nothing on `inventory.kills` -- the gates those count towards are for going in with a sword in your hand. `Camp` never touches an `Inventory`; the main scene pays it at Break camp.
- **A camp is never a second economy.** What it pays an hour is sampled off a real `Encounter.farm`, so every dial that moves what a tile is worth moves a camp with it and there is no figure here to keep in step. Nothing in `Camp` writes a number in gold.
- **The rates are worked out once and then it is arithmetic.** `make` samples at the moment camp is made; `earned` is a sum over the hours since. So an open window and a shut one pay exactly alike, a camp needs no `_process` and no banking on the way out, and gear changed afterwards (there is no way to, while camped) could not matter.
- **An endless run has no clock and no loss.** `advance` leaves `time_left` alone while `endless`; a run ends only by `stop()` or `give_up()`. Anything reading `time_left`, `remaining()` or `enemies` must ask `endless` first, and the lineup grows, so `lineup.size()` is its only honest end.
- **`_append_enemy` is the one place an enemy joins a fight,** from `tier_for(position)`. Ask `EnemyRoster.tier_of`, never "is it the last one": a run's elites come round forever. A boss is only ever rolled as a settlement's last enemy, never in a run.
- **The lineup is seeded from the cell; loot is not.** `loot_rng` / `orb_rng` are unseeded, so re-fighting a tile drops something else. Tests seed them. `always_drop` exists for tests and screenshots only.
- **A gear find that beat the chance rolls again, to `MOST_DROPS` (4) pieces off one body,** at the same chance and never `guaranteed` -- which is what stops a drop rate at `chance_for`'s ceiling looping for ever. **A certain roll never cascades:** the promised elite, a mimic's `MIMIC_ROLLS`, Trophy Hunter and `always_drop` each leave exactly one piece a roll. Lucky Wound still picks the better of two for the *first* find only, and the unique, the orb and the Magpie's purse-for-gear are separate single rolls.
- **`CombatScene._on_loot_dropped` is the only listener to `Encounter.loot_dropped`** and the only place autodiscard is applied; it forks into `loot_kept` / `loot_discarded`. A discarded find appears nowhere and is counted once under the verdict.
- **A unique is rolled beside the gear, on `unique_rng`, and leaves through `loot_dropped` like any find.** `uniques_after` is `NO_UNIQUES` (-1) until the main scene sets it, so a hand-built fight drops none and every test that counts finds counts what it always did. Nothing promises one but a chest: past the wait it is `UniqueTable.chance_for` alone. **The mimic is the one exception:** `_kill` tosses `MIMIC_UNIQUE` on `loot_rng`, and heads is one guaranteed unique (`UniqueTable.roll(..., guaranteed)`, no wait) and nothing else, tails is `MIMIC_ROLLS` (4) certain gear rolls; it never takes the ordinary unique roll. Autodiscard never applies to one.
- **Everything a unique adds to a blow goes into one sum, `_unique_more(automatic)`, applied once** -- never a multiplier of its own: metronome (swings) and berserk (clicks) +200%, home and glass_edge +100%, last_gasp +200% under 5 s, knucklebone, ascetic +15% a bare socket, momentum +2% a kill to +100%, packmule +1% a bag piece. Outside it: the Gambler's Die (a random factor on the finished blow, its own `gamble_rng`), Giant Slayer / Giantsbane (the larger, never both), crits. **A new damage unique adds a line to that sum.** The tuning consts stand together above it, so a card's sentence and the fight agree.
- **`wear(effects)` before `arm(stats)` before `start()`.** `wear` sets `effects` and reshapes a tile fight's lineup for two home clauses -- Grazing (`_lead` commons on the front, seeded from the cell, `enemies` + 2) and Flush out (first elite slot swapped to the head, open land only) -- and `tier_for` reads `_lead` / `_elite_first`, so it still agrees with `tier_in`. `arm` reads what needs a number: `overcrit`, `heartwood` (+1 s per `HEARTWOOD_ARMOUR` armour, `HEARTWOOD_MOST`), the four defence stats (block and time on hit converted out of tenths by `LootTable.seconds_of`), and `bare_sockets` / `bag_pieces`, which `Inventory.stats()` puts in. Riposte is read in `_struck_by`: a blow block takes to nothing is answered by `_strike(true)`. So are the Guard capstones: `afterimage` (a dodge wins back `min(AFTERIMAGE_SECONDS, wounds)`) and `shieldwall` (block doubled against an elite or boss, through `taken(hit, blocked)`); `second_wind` is read where the clock runs out in `advance`, once a fight (`_second_wind_used`), never `endless`. Setting `effects` by hand does all but the reshaping.
- **A wound is deepened, never stacked.** `arm` reads `bleed` (the mace's base stat); `_strike` sets `_bleed = maxf(_bleed, dealt * bleed / 100.0)` only where the body **survived** the blow, so a weapon swinging twice as fast cannot bleed twice as hard for free. It is cleared in `_advance_phase` beside `_struck`, so a body that gets back up (Restless dead) gets up whole.
- **`_wear_down(a_second, delta)` is the one path for damage nobody dealt** -- Heatstroke's share of the body's health and the wound's share of the blow, in that order, after `_swing_weapon`. `WAITING` only (a body walking in or going down takes none of it), and a death that way sets `_domino = false`, because it was nobody's blow.
- **Drop rate is the broad finder and is *added* to a narrow one where they meet** (`_kill`): `gold_find + drop_rate` for the purse, `orb_find + drop_rate` for `OrbTable.roll`, the way two global percents add. `gold_of` and `OrbTable.chance_for` are untouched, so what a body is worth -- and so a town's prices -- does not move.
- **`_kill()` is the one path to a death,** out of `_strike` because Heatstroke kills with no blow. Per-enemy flags `_struck` / `_crit_landed` / `_has_risen` are cleared in `_advance_phase`. **Restless dead rises in the slot it fell in** (`index` does not move, `enemy_coming` is re-sent), so the lineup and `KillPips` never change length; `kills()` is `index + _rose`. A home piece's second rule is asked with `_clause("<id>")`, which looks for `"<id>:<env>"`.
- **A worn unique's effect is one `if` in `Encounter`, beside the capstones',** and arrives in `effects` one entry a piece, so two of one ring are counted with `effects.count`. The first batch: `metronome` (a click lands nothing, the weapon's swing x3), `headsman` + `execute` (`EXECUTE_SHARE`, summed: 25%, 10%, 35%), `knucklebone` (clicks only; +2% a click within `KNUCKLE_WINDOW`, `KNUCKLE_MOST` a ring; a click that lands nothing still counts), `home:<env>` (x2 on that ground), `hourglass` (+1 s a kill, never past `seconds`, never for a BOSS, never `endless`), `tithe` (no `LootTable.roll` at all; purse x3, x5 for two). **Anything new that touches the clock must ask `endless` and must not be able to bank time.**
- **Nothing strikes a clock that is not there, or a wall.** `_be_struck` asks `strikes`, `endless` and `WALL_NAME` first; time on hit asks `endless`. A blow is sized off `base_hp`, so it grows with the walk and steps with every wall exactly as health does, and `HIT_TIER` against `ATTACK_EVERY` keeps every tier's seconds-a-second equal. `test_combat._test_enemy_strikes` pins the user's example (100 s against 90% armour and 10 s of block is nothing), a bare first ring still won at 8 clicks a second, and the frontier past the wall won in the farmed rares and lost without their defence. `HIT_SECONDS`, `ARMOUR_K` and `DODGE_K` (50 each: 50 is half, 450 is 90%) are the dials.
- **The view answers `player_hit` in `_on_player_hit`:** the enemy plays `attack` (and `_on_enemy_hit` plays `hurt` only while it is not mid-attack, so a flinch never cuts a swing off), the clock flashes (`CLOCK_STRUCK`), and "-2.4s", "Dodge" or "Block" rises over the hero's head (`STRUCK_HEIGHT`, outlined). Everything that floats off a fight goes through `_float_text`.
- **Orbs never enter `_drops`:** the counter, discard, `bag_room` and autodiscard know nothing about them. They roll on their own generator beside gear, not against it. `DropsView.fill(items, orbs)` only draws them, as a row of mouse-deaf `OrbSlot`s under the squares, and only the verdict hands them over -- so every `_result_drops.fill` passes `fight.orbs`.
- **The verdict reads top to bottom:** the word, one row of sums (coin, gem, skull -- bare numbers, no `+`; the kills are there for a tile fight too), then the finds and the orbs on the bag's `TextPanel`, then the autodiscard footnote and the way out, which `_on_finished` picks -- the word **Collect** (`_collect`) for a won tile and an ended run, the back arrow beside **Retry** (`_lost_row`) for a lost one. Retry emits `retry`, and the main scene's `_on_combat_retry` leaves through `_on_combat_finished(false, ..)` and comes back in through `_on_chart_pressed`, so a second go banks and opens like any other. Only a loss has a second line ("Out of time"); a win and a run say nothing under the word.
- **Everything in the HUD is placed by `_place_corners` every frame, not anchored:** it is scaled by `ui_scale`, which anchors know nothing about. Columns are centred, so a wider elite or boss bar grows evenly either side.
- **HUD words stand on the backdrop with no panel:** use `_hud_label` (bone with a dark outline) and `BAR_BORDER`, never a plain `Label`.
- **The nameplate is dressed once per enemy, in `_dress_nameplate` (from `_on_enemy_coming`), never in `_refresh`:** the name in `TIER_COLOUR` between `TIER_MARK`s (a bone skull for an elite, a gilded crown at 2x for a boss), an elite's `Encounter.enemy_title()` in front of its name, a boss's under it in the body font with the name at `BOSS_FONT` (32, Pixellari's one other clean size). A boss's plate pops in white (`_plate_pop`, which `_place_corners` multiplies into the panel's scale) at `Settings.Anim.DEFAULT` only. **A title is for show:** bounties and everything else count `enemy_name()`.
- **Tint the loot counter's styleboxes, not the Button:** `modulate` would take the icon and the count with it (`_tint_loot_button`, one copy per `UITheme.STATES`).
- **Colours ramp, they do not switch:** `_clock_color` and the counter's face are both shares of the whole, so retuning `Encounter.SECONDS` or the bag's cap retunes them.
- **`Palette.GOLD` is the unique item step:** the verdict's gold total is in the panel's own colour behind a coin.
- **The clock bar is `KillPips.WIDTH` less its border,** so pips and clock are one column to the pixel.
- **`Settings.animations` thins the view and never the fight:** at `LOW` a body throws one coin and one gem, a number neither pops nor drifts, and `Juice` shakes and freezes nothing; at `NONE` nothing is thrown or written and no body bursts -- but `_show_xp` still emits `xp_absorbed` (at once), and gold, finds and orbs had already gone out by their own signals. Anything new that is only for show asks the level; anything that carries an amount must not be behind it.
- **`Juice.hit_stop`: the latest call wins,** so a kill's freeze cannot cut short an elite find's slow-motion.
- **`arm` clamps crit chance to `CRIT_CAP`;** crit damage is what a crit *adds* (50 = half again). An auto-swing only lands while an enemy is standing, so nothing banks through a walk-in.
