## Combat code (`Scenes/Combat/`)
A tile is taken by beating a lineup of enemies against a clock; a taken tile can be farmed endlessly with no clock, or **camped** -- that same run, played for the hours the game is shut on the best tile taken, a full eight hours paying what half an hour of active play does. **The dungeon** is the third kind: a clock and a lineup that never ends, in depths of fifteen floors, and a depth is won only by killing the Gollux at the foot of it. **Read `DESIGN.md` here before changing the HUD's layout, what drops show as, or the fight profiles** -- it holds the why.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `encounter.gd` (`Encounter`) | The rules, no nodes. `for_tile(cell, env, variant, chest)` rolls a lineup seeded from the cell; `farm(...)` is the same fight `endless`. `PROFILES` (keyed by `MapBuilder.area_variant`): `ORDINARY` is ten enemies in 30 s ending on an elite, `SETTLEMENT` fifteen in 60 s with an elite every fifth and a boss last, `CHEST` a lone `MIMIC`. `for_wall(cell)` is the ice wall: `WALL` profile, the lone `WALL_NAME` in 60 s on the `ice` backdrop, health `hp_of * WALL_HP` and no growth term of its own -- `hp_of(.., WALL_GROWTH)` already steps once per wall, and a wall's ring counts none of itself, so the second wall is `WALL_GROWTH` times the first on top of the walk between them (`WALL_HP` is the dial for how hard a wall is). **From the fourth wall on** (`LATE_WALL_FROM`) a wall is instead `LATE_WALL_BODIES` (150) ordinary bodies of the ring just inside it, `base_hp * hp_tuning`. `arm(stats)` reads the player's stats once; `hit()` is a click (`click()` is the hand's, under `CLICK_CAP`: only the scene calls it), `advance(delta)` runs the clock, auto-swings, lets the enemy strike (`_be_struck`) and then wears the body down (`_wear_down`, the bleed), `stop()` ends a run, `kills()` is its score. **Strikes:** while `strikes` (the main scene's, off in a hand-built fight) a standing enemy hits every `ATTACK_EVERY[tier]` (common 1 s, elite 2, boss 4) for `hit_of(enemy, cell)` = `HIT_SECONDS * HIT_GROWTH ^ steps * HIT_WALL_GROWTH ^ walls_inside(cell) * HIT_TIER[tier]` seconds of clock; `_struck_by(hit)` rolls `dodge_chance()`, else takes `taken(hit)` = the hit less armour's share (`rating / (rating + ARMOUR_K)`, the same share of any blow) less block, adds it to `wounds`, and emits `player_hit(taken, dodged, blocked)`. A landed hit of the player's gives back `min(time_on_hit, wounds)`. Signals per enemy, hit, death and drop (`loot_dropped`, `gold_dropped`, `orb_dropped`, `xp_dropped`), then `won` / `lost`. `tier_in(slot)` is the tier both `KillPips` and `HealthBar` read. Health is `BASE_HP * HP_GROWTH ^ steps * LAND_GROWTH ^ walls_inside(cell) * hp_modifier` -- **every wall behind a cell multiplies its bodies by `LAND_GROWTH` (10)**, while the walls themselves step by `WALL_GROWTH` (2.2), passed to `base_hp` / `hp_of` as `step`, and a blow (`hit_of`) grows on its own slower curve (`HIT_GROWTH` 1.1 a ring, `HIT_WALL_GROWTH` 1.5 a wall); which is what keeps the land a fallen wall opens from dying to one click; gold is `base_gold(cell) * hp_modifier` and does **not** step with it, and `base_gold` is `gold_at_steps(distance)` so a price can be quoted in bodies with no cell to point at (`TownPrices`) |

No nodes; the main scene makes one per fight. `add_loot/gold/orb/xp` (the Broken Sword's and the first orb's flags set as they land), `add_kill(enemy)` (a body for the bounty boards, `BountyBoard.count_kill`, then `bounty_counted(enemy, have, need)` -- a body past `need` is said by neither; the bounty's monster refused only for falling on land shallower than the posting's `level` is `bounty_too_low(enemy, level)` instead), `autodiscarded`, `discard` (false for a piece no longer in the bag), `bank_kills` |
| `drops_view.gd` (`DropsView`) | A fight's finds as a grid of `ItemSlot`s on the bag's light `TextPanel`, with the orbs it is handed in a row under them, one find openable into an `ItemDetails` block; used by both the verdict panel and the loot popup. A camp is the tile's farm run played actively and cut to `IDLE_SHARE`: `rates(fight)` plays an armed endless `Encounter` clicked at `CLICK_RATE` (5 a second) for a sample (`SAMPLE_SECONDS` 120, on to `SAMPLE_KILLS` 5 bodies, `SAMPLE_MOST` 1800 either way, at `SAMPLE_STEP`) and hands back that share of what it earned a second in gold, experience and bodies -- **a full camp (`MAX_SECONDS`, 8 h) pays half an hour of active play**. `make(cell, place, fight, at)` is the camp -- `SINCE`, `CELL`, `PLACE`, `MOST` and those three rates -- and `earned(camp, at)` is rate times seconds, clamped to its `MOST` and never negative, with `full` saying the cap is what stopped it. `cell_of`, `spell_time` |

Top-right: Terminate (a run: `stop()`; a tile fight: `give_up()`, a loss whose verdict says "Given up"). `sky_at(hour)` the sky from the player's clock (`SKY_HOURS`), whose light (`SKY_LIGHT`) is a multiply on every layer but the sky. **A settlement is held, not walked through** (`_walks_on`: its `Encounter.PROFILES` entry is `SETTLEMENT`): the hero stays idle, the enemies come to him and nothing scrolls. **What a body throws lies on `_ground_drops`**, which `_scroll` slides with the ground; a piece the hero reaches (`drawn_size` past his feet) is picked up at once (`_pick_up`: its throw's tween, kept as the `THROWN` meta, killed, then `_fly_to_counter`, which moves it to the scene and leaves its beam on the ground). `hud_top` is how far down the top-centre column stands (`HUD_MARGIN`, or under the character panel on a window held upright: the main scene's `_fight_top`, set beside `xp_target`). **Sounds:** `weapon_kind` (`LootTable.KINDS`, "" bare-handed; set before `begin`) picks a hit's blow -- `BLUNT_HIT` for a `BLUNT` kind, the punch otherwise -- and its crit, which plays instead of it: `BLUNT_CRIT` bare-handed or blunt, `SLASH_CRIT` for a blade (the user's ruling), with `GENERAL_CRIT` over it (`_crit_layer`, the user's pairing, 2026-10-06). A blow that took time plays `STRUCK_SOUND` (dodged or blocked, nothing); a find, every coin of a purse (held back by its place in the burst, `THROW_STAGGER`; one at no animation) and an orb play as they land (`_land`: `THROW_TIME` on, on a tween so slow motion and a tip hold it; at once at no animation), a find by `drop_sound_of` (a unique its own, every other piece `item_drop`; an orb `orb_drop`: the user's, 2026-10-06; every find, coin and orb pitched at random within `DROP_PITCH`); experience as it reaches the bar (`xp_absorbed`); a lost fight's verdict `DEFEAT_SOUND`, and a won one nothing (its fanfare was annoying: the user's ruling, 2026-10-02). Takes of one sound are one `Juice.takes` pool. |

## Rules and gotchas
- **The fight's `HUD` is drawn over everything else in the fight** (`z_index` `RenderingServer.CANVAS_ITEM_Z_MAX`, the user's, 2026-10-03): a find, its beam, a coin, a gem, a number or a burst carries a `z_index` of its own, and never one that reaches it. `test_combat` holds a beam under it.
- **The dungeon (`Encounter.for_dungeon(won)`, `dungeon`, `first_floor`, `depth()`, `cleared()`) measures damage and nothing else,** and every rule of it is one `if dungeon` in `Encounter`: a floor's health is `BASE_HP * DUNGEON_GROWTH ^ floor * DUNGEON_TIER_HP[tier]`, **Gollux's `gollux_hp()` (the second ice wall's, `for_wall`, to the nearest `GOLLUX_ROUND` 500k, the user's 2026-10-03) times `DUNGEON_GROWTH` a floor past the first depth's last**, and nothing of the roster's sizes, a tile's modifiers or a world's curses (`_health_of`, which is handed the position when `_take_curses` sizes bodies again); `tier_for` counts in **floors** (`first_floor + position`), an elite every fifth and Gollux every fifteenth for ever; nothing strikes; `_kill` returns before anything is rolled, so it pays **nothing** -- no gear, purse, experience, unique, orb or Hourglass second -- **but a rune** once the third wall has opened them (`runes_drop`, `RuneTable.roll` off `loot_rng`, `rune_dropped`, which `CombatScene` throws in the rare's beam and passes on as `rune_gained`); the clock runs as it does in any fight, walk-ins and deaths included (the user's, 2026-10-03); and the clock running out is `_finish(true)`, an end and not a loss. **A depth is fifteen floors and is won only when its Gollux has fallen:** `depth()` is `(first_floor + index) / enemies + 1`, so it moves as `index` passes him and never for a floor short of him, and `cleared()` is how many this descent has won. `for_dungeon(won)` begins at the top of the depth under the ones already won, with a whole clock, and a descent that kills Gollux goes on into the next depth on what is left of it. A click lands as it does anywhere (the user's ruling). **`endless` stays false:** anything that asks `endless` to mean "no clock" is right about the dungeon, and the four places that mean "the lineup grows" ask `dungeon` beside it (`_advance_phase`, `wear`, `KillPips.show_fight`, the view's Terminate). Reasoning and the turned-down shapes: `DESIGN.md`.
- **The view of it (`CombatScene`):** the backdrop is the cave's seven layers (`Assets/Area/cave/<n>.png`, **1 the nearest and 7 the flat dark; the pack's 0 is all of them put together, a preview, and is not exported** -- drawn as a layer it hid the rest and there was no parallax at all). `_build_cave` lays 7 down to 2 behind the fighters and `_build` adds **1 after them, so the nearest rock stands in front of the hero and the monsters**; each slides at its share of `WALK_SPEED` while the next floor walks in (`_scroll`, the farthest still and the nearest at the full pace) -- **the one scroll every fight has**: a place's layers go by the same way, the hero playing `walk` until the enemy stands (`_on_enemy_spawned`), none of it at `Settings.Anim.NONE`. **The fighters stand at `_ground()`, never at `GROUND`:** `CAVE_GROUND` down there, because the cave's floor lies lower than a backdrop's grass band and at `GROUND` everyone floated. The heading's gold half is "Depth n", rewritten in `_refresh`; no loot counter; Terminate is `stop()`; the verdict's word is the depth, its second line whether Gollux fell, no finds, and **Leave** in Collect's place. The main scene opens it with `_on_dungeon_pressed`, from the world's cave's Enter cave (`Scenes/CLAUDE.md`) -- no ledger, no board hears of a body, none of its kills are banked -- and `_bank_depths` writes `inventory.dungeon_depth` where the descent won any, and `inventory.dungeon_floors` (`first_floor + index`, the leaderboard's score) where it went further than ever, on the way out of it and out of the game.
- **A tile's modifiers reach a fight through `for_tile(.., mods)` / `farm(.., mods)` and nowhere else,** and a fight nobody tells has none. `_take_mods` shapes the fight right after the profile and before the lineup is rolled; what they do to the player's numbers and what they pay is the tail of `arm()`, added to the finders so `_kill` has no second path. **`farm` keeps only `TileMods.farmable`:** a run has no clock and is never struck, so a clock or strike modifier there would be pay for nothing. A chest and the wall carry none. Barren and Gilded are asked for by id in `_kill`.
- **A curse is one `if` on `Curses.effect(id) in effects`, like a worn unique's,** and the ones that shape the fight are taken on once in `wear` (`_take_curses`, `_cursed`): Iron Foes and the Bloodthirst add to the tile's own shares, Short Days cuts every clock with one, and a body's health is sized again through `_health_of` -- **only under a curse that moves health, so a fight built by hand keeps the health it was given**. `_health_of` is the one place a body's starting health is worked out; the ice wall answers there to `WALL_HP` and the Long Winter and to nothing else.
- **A finished chance is halved or doubled through `_lifted(rate, factor)`,** never by adding points: Lean Pickings hands every gear roll `_gear_rate()` (the purse, the orbs and the unique roll keep the full `drop_rate`), the Thick Fog hands the unique roll twice its chance. `_lean(item)` is the curse's pay -- a find falls +1 or +2 (`LEAN_PLUS`, `Item.ascend`) -- drawn on `loot_rng` **only under the curse**, so nobody else's loot stream moves; never the Broken Sword. **Hard Lessons is a "less" too:** `_kill` multiplies the finished experience by `LESSONS_XP` after every `xp_more` has been added, so a camp, which samples a real run, is taught as slowly.
- **A curse that is a unique's rule made a world's adds to the unique, in the unique's own place** (the user's ruling): Berserker's World is a line in `unique_more` beside the Band's, the Glass World is `_glass()` -- how many things are spending the clock -- so the clock is `GLASS_CLOCK ^ n` and the damage `GLASS_MORE * n`, and Pacifist Hands stills the click where the Metronome does. **`swings()` is the one answer to whether the weapon swings on its own** (`_swing_weapon`). Pacifist Hands' swings are in `Inventory.stats()`, not here, so the character page sees them.
- **Raw Finds and the Homeland are two more hands on `_kill`:** `_raw(item)` strips a gear find to a bare common (never a unique, and before `_lean`, so a raw find can still fall +1); a fight abroad (`Curses.effect(HOMELAND)` without `HOME_PREFIX + env`) rolls no gear exactly as a Gilded tile does -- **but never a mimic or the wall, which are no land's** -- and at home `arm` adds `HOME_RARITY` and the unique roll is lifted `HOME_UNIQUES` times, multiplied with the Thick Fog's.
- **A Restless camp is still arithmetic:** `Camp.make` reads the curse off the fight's `effects`, doubles the two rates it pays and writes `MOST` (2 h) into the camp; `earned` clamps to its `MOST`, and one without fills at `MAX_SECONDS`.
- **`walk_in` is the fight's own** (`WALK_IN`, doubled in a Mire, cut by the gear's `spawn_speed` in `arm` -- to nothing at 100%, so `arm` works from the const and the view guards the division): tests that play an unmodified fight still step by the const.
- **A camp pays gold and experience and nothing else:** no gear, no orbs, no bodies for a bounty board and nothing on `inventory.kills` -- the gates those count towards are for going in with a sword in your hand. `Camp` never touches an `Inventory`; the main scene pays it at start-up.
- **A camp is never a second economy.** What it pays an hour is sampled off a real `Encounter.farm`, so every dial that moves what a tile is worth moves a camp with it and there is no figure here to keep in step. Nothing in `Camp` writes a number in gold.
- **The rates are worked out once and then it is arithmetic.** `make` samples at start-up, from the gear the save holds -- the gear the player shut the game in -- and `earned` is a sum over the hours since `Inventory.saved_at`.
- **An endless run has no clock and no loss.** `advance` leaves `time_left` alone while `endless`; a run ends only by `stop()` or `give_up()`. Anything reading `time_left`, `remaining()` or `enemies` must ask `endless` first, and the lineup grows, so `lineup.size()` is its only honest end.
- **`_append_enemy` is the one place an enemy joins a fight,** from `tier_for(position)`. Ask `EnemyRoster.tier_of`, never "is it the last one": a run's elites come round forever. A boss is only ever rolled as a settlement's last enemy, never in a run.
- **`even_loot` (dev, set by the main scene from `Settings.even_loot_on()`) replaces a body's gear and unique rolls** with one find at `EVEN_LOOT_CHANCE` (1/3), its rarity drawn evenly from common to unique (`_even_find`, `LootTable.roll(.., forced)`); a chest keeps its own rules, and the purse and the orb are rolled as ever.
- **The lineup is seeded from the cell; loot is not.** `loot_rng` / `orb_rng` are unseeded, so re-fighting a tile drops something else. Tests seed them. `always_drop` exists for tests and screenshots only.
- **A gear find that beat the chance rolls again, to `MOST_DROPS` (4) pieces off one body,** at the same chance and never `guaranteed` -- which is what stops a drop rate at `chance_for`'s ceiling looping for ever. **A certain roll never cascades:** the promised sword's kill, a mimic's `MIMIC_ROLLS`, Trophy Hunter and `always_drop` each leave exactly one piece a roll. Lucky Wound still picks the better of two for the *first* find only, and the unique, the orb and the Magpie's purse-for-gear are separate single rolls.
- **`CombatScene._on_loot_dropped` is the only listener to `Encounter.loot_dropped`** and the only place autodiscard is applied (`autodiscard`, a Callable asked of the whole find: `Inventory.leaves_behind`, the level's rule and the loot filter);
- **A farm run carries the runes on its tile** (`RuneTable`): their modifiers ride in `mods` with the tile's own, `farm(.., deeper)` sets the Depths before the first body (each `MapBuilder.LEVEL_TILES` rings' `HP_GROWTH` on a body's health and `GOLD_GROWTH` on its purse, and a level on its experience and on what drops, `_level()`), and `ascended` lets a find fall ascended as Lean Pickings' do (`_lean`). `forget_mod(id)` takes one listing of a worn-off modifier out of a run, its health, blows and elite pace (back to `_base_elite_every`) from the next body on; what it paid goes at the next `arm`. A camp carries none of it. it forks into `loot_kept` / `loot_discarded`. A discarded find appears nowhere and is counted once under the verdict.
- **The promised finds (`PROMISED`, `promised`, `kills_before`, `_due(promise, rng)`; the rule is `Scenes/CLAUDE.md`'s).** The body going down is lifetime kill `kills_before + index + 1`. `_due` draws one chance in the kills the promise has left, on the find's own generator (`loot_rng` the sword, `orb_rng` the orbs, `unique_rng`, `stone_rng`), and **only for a promise owed**, so a fight nobody tells rolls exactly as it always did. Past its last kill inside one fight it is certain. A promise is erased as its find falls, luck's too: any gear while the sword is owed is the sword, an Augmentation off the table and a unique off the pool spend theirs. `guarantee_elite` (an elite's certain piece) is set by nothing in the game, like `always_drop`.
- **A unique is rolled beside the gear, on `unique_rng`, and leaves through `loot_dropped` like any find.** `uniques_after` is `NO_UNIQUES` (-1) until the main scene sets it, so a hand-built fight drops none and every test that counts finds counts what it always did. Nothing promises one but a chest and the first (`PROMISED`'s `UNIQUE`: `UniqueTable.FIRST_UNIQUE`, off `unique_rng`, never a mimic's body, and spent by a unique luck finds first): past the wait it is `UniqueTable.chance_for` alone. **The mimic is the one exception:** `_kill` tosses `MIMIC_UNIQUE` on `loot_rng`, and heads is one guaranteed unique (`UniqueTable.roll(..., guaranteed)`, no wait) and nothing else, tails is `MIMIC_ROLLS` (4) certain gear rolls; it never takes the ordinary unique roll. Autodiscard never applies to one.
- **The weapon's lines are one sum, `gear_more(automatic, big, first)`, applied in `_strike` just before `unique_more` and never inside it** (a gear line is not a unique): Click Damage on a click, Swing Damage on a swing (a riposte is one), Elite Damage where `big`, First Blow Damage where `first` (`_blows` was 0). **Double Strike** doubles a blow beside the Band's and the Chalice's, drawn on `crit_rng` only while `double_strike` is above 0. Both chances `arm` reads (`double_strike`, `elite_chance`) are clamped to 100. **Elite Chance promotes** (`_promote(at)`): a common comes on as an elite of `env` (`EnemyRoster.pick`, health by `_health_of`), on its own unseeded `elite_rng`, drawn only while there is a chance; the lineup already rolled once on the first `arm` (`_promoted`, the current body's `hp` with it), and every body `_append_enemy` adds after. Never the dungeon; the wall and a mimic are bosses and never asked.
- **`average_blow(automatic)` is `_strike` averaged** (crits, `gear_more` of a common's non-first blow, Double Strike, `unique_more`, the Band's tenth click, the Chalice's doubled crit, the Gambler's Die's mean; nothing of Giant Slayer, Elite or First Blow Damage, or a streak), for the character page's and the character panel's damage per second (`per_second()`: the average swing times the swings, the bleed and the burn, 0 where nothing swings) and per click: **a change to what a blow deals in `_strike` changes it too** (`test_combat` holds a few cases).
- **The helmet's, the body's and the offhand's lines (2026-10-07), all read in `arm`:** `blow_delay` (`_advance_phase` starts `_attack` at minus that share of `_attack_every()` as a body takes its stand), `elite_ward` (a share less of what got through from an elite or a boss, in `_struck_by`), `tile_ward` (`_tile_factor` gives back that share of a tile's factor on armour, dodge, block, time on hit, swing and walk-in, `_softened` of its `hit` and `attack`, and `arm`'s clock `more` of a Dusk's seconds; Thick-skinned's `hp` once in `_shape`; the counts -- Horde, Sparse, Elite Ground -- and what a tile pays are untouched), `less_health` (`_health_of` is `_raw_health_of` less it, on every body: the wall's and the dungeon's too), `camp_more` (`Camp.make`'s pay), `extra_enemies` (`_take_extra`: commons put on or taken off the front of a tile fight, from `hash(["extra", cell])` so a tile fields the same ones; never the last body, a run, the dungeon, a wall or a mimic), `thorns` (`_wear_down` of `damage` times it after every blow not dodged), `recoup` (a share of each blow owed back over `RECOUP_SECONDS`, `_recouping`, paid in `advance` before the clock's verdict), `parry` (`_parry`, added to the next blow's crit chance under `CRIT_CAP` and spent by it), `burn` (`_burn`, the deepest, lit again by every blow a body survives, for `BURN_SECONDS`; cleared with the body) and `time_on_block` (a blow the block stops whole wins it back). **Everything that wins clock back goes through `_win_back`, out of `wounds` and never more.** The three "less" lines stop at `WARD_MOST` (75), the recoup at 100. **`_shape` is the once-a-fight say over the rolled lineup** (`_promoted`): the count, then health sized again only where the gear moved it (a hand-built fight keeps its own), then the elite chance.
- **Everything a unique adds to a blow goes into one sum, `unique_more(automatic)`, applied once** -- never a multiplier of its own: metronome (swings), berserk (clicks), home, glass_edge, knucklebone, ascetic (a bare socket), packmule (a bag piece), patchwork, purist and brawler at their rank's numbers (`_dial`), last_gasp +200% in its rank's window, momentum +2% a kill to +100%. Outside it: the Gambler's Die (a random factor on the finished blow, its own `gamble_rng`), Giant Slayer, crits, and the Bulwark's answer at its rank's share. **A new damage unique adds a line to that sum.** Its ranked numbers are its own row's (`UniqueTable`) and the fixed ones stand together above it, so a card's sentence and the fight agree.
- **The Serpent's Eye is crit chance, not damage, so it stays out of that sum:** `_serpent` grows by its rank's step a Serpent's Eye (one on each doll counts twice, the user's ruling) for every blow that does not crit, is added to `crit_chance` under `CRIT_CAP` in `_strike`, and a crit empties it (at IV leaves `SERPENT_KEPT` of it). It lasts the whole fight, across bodies.
- **Skill nodes drop on a roll of their own** (`SkillTree.roll`, on `stone_rng`), after the orbs and out through `loot_dropped` like gear, only while `stone_drops` is on -- the main scene turns it on once the first sword has dropped, and a fight nobody tells drops none -- and never while the first node is owed (`NODE in promised`): that one is `SkillTree.first` on its promised kill, no roll at all. Neither a curse nor a unique that keeps gear from falling keeps a stone. A capstone's effect reaches `effects` the way a unique's does (`Skills.effects`, once each).
- **`wear(effects, ranks)` before `arm(stats)` before `start()`.** `wear` sets `effects` and `ranks` (unique id -> rank, `Achievements.ranks`; a unique it does not name is rank I, which is every hand-built fight) and takes the curses on -- after the Dreadmask (`_take_dread`, once, `_dreaded`): its rank's number of commons, less one a wall inside the tile, taken off the front of a tile fight's rolled lineup (with their `health`; elites, the boss and the last body stay, and `enemies` follows), never `endless` or `dungeon`. **`CombatScene.auto_collect`** (the main scene sets it while the Nightwalkers are worn or distant charting is open, never for a run) leaves a won tile fight by itself `AUTO_COLLECT_SECONDS` after the verdict, through the same `_leave` as Collect (`Juice.pop_out` leaves once, so a press first wins); it is a Timer child, so a tip, which disables the fight, holds it too. **`arm` may run again mid-fight** (the main scene's `_rearm`, after the gear changes in the bag): everything in it starts from the stats, and the clock moves by the change in the gear's seconds (`_clock_more`), never refilled. `arm` reads what needs a number: `overcrit` (its rank's times), `heartwood` (+1 s per `HEARTWOOD_ARMOUR` armour, its rank's most), the four defence stats (block and time on hit converted out of tenths by `LootTable.seconds_of`), and `bare_sockets` / `bag_pieces`, which `Inventory.stats()` puts in. Riposte is read in `_struck_by`: a blow block takes to nothing is answered by `_strike(true, true)`, at the Bulwark's rank's share. So are the Guard capstones: `afterimage` (a dodge wins back `min(AFTERIMAGE_SECONDS, wounds)`) and `shieldwall` (block doubled against an elite or boss, through `taken(hit, blocked)`); `second_wind` and the Worry Stone's `worry_stone` are read where the clock runs out in `advance`, once a fight each and the two adding up (`_saves_used`), never `endless`. `beginners_luck` is read in `arm` (the raw crit chance is `BEGINNERS_LUCK`, so the Overflowing Chalice gets nothing) and in `_strike` (the Serpent's Eye adds nothing to it). Setting `effects` by hand does all but take the curses on, at rank I.
- **A wound is deepened, never stacked.** `arm` reads `bleed` (the mace's base stat); `_strike` sets `_bleed = maxf(_bleed, dealt * bleed / 100.0)` only where the body **survived** the blow, so a weapon swinging twice as fast cannot bleed twice as hard for free. It is cleared in `_advance_phase` beside `_blows` -- unless the body died of it under a rank IV Butcher's Cleaver, which hands it to the next (`_bled_out`).
- **`_wear_down(a_second, delta)` is the one path for damage nobody dealt** -- the wound's share of the blow, after `_swing_weapon`, the torch's burn, the body's thorns, and the armour a rank IV Spiked Helm sends back (`_spikes_back`). `WAITING` only (a body walking in or going down takes none of it), and a death that way sets `_domino = false`, because it was nobody's blow.
- **Drop rate is the broad finder and is *added* to a narrow one where they meet** (`_kill`): `gold_find + drop_rate` for the purse, `orb_find + drop_rate` for `OrbTable.roll`, the way two global percents add. `gold_of` and `OrbTable.chance_for` are untouched, so what a body is worth -- and so a town's prices -- does not move.
- **`_kill(swung)` is the one path to a death,** out of `_strike` because a wound and Dominoes' carry kill with no blow; `swung` (the weapon's own swing felled it) is what a rank IV Metronome answers with the next swing ready. Per-enemy flags `_blows` / `_crit_kill` / `_walled` / `_bled_out` are cleared in `_advance_phase`; `_crit_kill` is whether the last blow critted, so Lucky Wound pays only where a crit felled the body (a wound's death clears it). `kills()` is `index`. **Dominoes carries `_carried`** -- its rank's share of a one-blow kill's overkill -- and deals it as the next body takes its stand (`_take_carried`); a body it fells is a one-blow kill and passes its own leftover on at the same share; an elite or a boss takes none of it (half at IV). Cleave's `_overkill`, and a rank IV Headsman's executed health, come off a body as it comes on and never fell one. Grazing, Flush out, Heatstroke, the Frozen clock, Giantsbane and Restless dead went with the Pilgrim's set (2026-09-29).
- **A worn unique's effect is one `if` in `Encounter`, beside the capstones',** and arrives in `effects` one entry a piece, so two of one ring are counted with `effects.count`. The first batch: `metronome` (a click lands nothing, the weapon's swing its rank's times), `headsman` + `execute` (`EXECUTE_SHARE`, a tenth, and the Headsman's rank's share, summed), `knucklebone` (clicks only; its rank's step a click within `KNUCKLE_WINDOW`, up to its rank's most a ring; a click that lands nothing still counts), `home:<env>` (its rank's times on that ground), `hourglass` (its rank's seconds a kill, never past `seconds`, never for a BOSS, never `endless`), `tithe` (no `LootTable.roll` at all; the purse its rank's times, two adding). **Anything new that touches the clock must ask `endless` and must not be able to bank time.**
- **A rank IV line is one `if` beside the rule it grows,** asking `_peak(id)` -- or `_home_peak()` on a home piece's own ground -- beside whether the piece is worn, its numbers consts after `MAGPIE_CHANCE` (`SERPENT_KEPT` ... `CHALICE_TWICE`). One that rolls draws only under its piece at IV (the Chalice on `crit_rng`, the second unique chance on `unique_rng`), so nobody else's rolls move; the clock ones (Rimeplate, Last Gasp, Hourglass, Heartwood) ask `endless` like the rest.
- **Nothing strikes a clock that is not there, or a wall.** `_be_struck` asks `strikes`, `endless` and `WALL_NAME` first; time on hit asks `endless`. A blow grows by `HIT_GROWTH` a ring and `HIT_WALL_GROWTH` a wall, far slower than health, because armour is a share and could not keep up with health's curve, and `HIT_TIER` against `ATTACK_EVERY` keeps every tier's seconds-a-second equal. `test_combat._test_enemy_strikes` pins the user's example (100 s against 90% armour and 10 s of block is nothing). `HIT_SECONDS`, `HIT_GROWTH`, `HIT_WALL_GROWTH`, `ARMOUR_K` and `DODGE_K` (50 each: 50 is half, 450 is 90%) are the dials.
- **The view answers `player_hit` in `_on_player_hit`:** the enemy plays `attack` (and `_on_enemy_hit` plays `hurt` only while it is not mid-attack, so a flinch never cuts a swing off), the clock flashes (`CLOCK_STRUCK`), and "-2.4s", "Dodge" or "Block" rises over the hero's head (`STRUCK_HEIGHT`, outlined). Everything that floats off a fight goes through `_float_text`.
- **Orbs never enter `_drops`:** the counter, discard, `bag_room` and autodiscard know nothing about them. They roll on their own generator beside gear, not against it. `DropsView.fill(items, orbs)` only draws them, as a row of `OrbSlot`s under the squares that press nothing and put the bag's own `OrbCard` up beside them on hover (top level, so it stands over the panel at its scale), and only the verdict hands them over -- so every `_result_drops.fill` passes `fight.orbs`.
- **The verdict is a `UITheme.titled_panel` with no X** (the word on its green bar, `LightButton`s on its cream), popped in, counted up and revealed by `Juice` in `_on_finished`, celebrated unless lost, and left through `_leave` (Collect, the arrow, Retry and Escape all shrink it away first; `_on_back_pressed` itself is still immediate, which is what the tests call). `_centre_result` keeps it under `hud_bottom()` where the window has room.
- **The verdict reads top to bottom:** the word -- on a red bar for a lost tile (`UITheme.danger_bar`), the green one otherwise -- one row of sums (coin, gem, the kills' crossed swords `KILLS_MARK` -- bare numbers, no `+`; the kills are there for a tile fight too; the skull they wore was a unique's icon and a curse's cost), then the finds and the orbs on the bag's `TextPanel`, then the autodiscard footnote and the way out, which `_on_finished` picks -- the word **Collect** (`_collect`) for a won tile and an ended run, the back arrow beside **Retry** (`_lost_row`) for a lost one. Retry emits `retry`, and the main scene's `_on_combat_retry` leaves through `_on_combat_finished(false, ..)` and comes back in through `_on_chart_pressed`, so a second go banks and opens like any other. Only a loss has a second line ("Out of time"); a win and a run say nothing under the word.
- **A fighter is `_actor_height(view)` tall: the lesser of `ACTOR_HEIGHT` of the view's height and `ACTOR_WIDE` of its width**, so held upright the two stay apart; across a monitor the height is always the lesser. **It snaps to `_snap(view)`: the backdrop's pixel, or half of it on a window held upright** (`UITheme.narrow`), whose backdrop is blown up to cover its height and would otherwise draw nobody smaller than a giant. The HUD's corners keep to `UITheme.safe_rect`. **Held upright the fight stands mid-window:** `_ground()` is `GROUND_UPRIGHT` (0.6) there and `_art_ground()` (`GROUND` or `CAVE_GROUND`) everywhere else, every layer is drawn `_lift(view)` higher to meet it (`_add_layer`; none across a monitor), each layer's own bottom `UNDERFOOT_ROWS` repeat under it to the window's foot (`_underfoot`, a child sprite in `_layers`, so it slides with its layer), and the nameplate stands `PLATE_UNDER` under the feet. Anything placed on the ground asks `_ground()`, never `GROUND`.
- **Everything in the HUD is placed by `_place_corners` every frame, not anchored:** it is scaled by `ui_scale`, which anchors know nothing about. Columns are centred, so a wider elite or boss bar grows evenly either side.
- **HUD words stand on the backdrop with no panel:** use `_hud_label` (bone with a dark outline) and `BAR_BORDER`, never a plain `Label`.
- **The nameplate is dressed once per enemy, in `_dress_nameplate` (from `_on_enemy_coming`), never in `_refresh`:** the name in `TIER_COLOUR` between `TIER_MARK`s (a bone skull for an elite, a gilded crown at 2x for a boss), an elite's `Encounter.enemy_title()` in front of its name, a boss's under it in the body font with the name at `BOSS_FONT` (32, Pixellari's one other clean size). A boss's plate pops in white (`_plate_pop`, which `_place_corners` multiplies into the panel's scale) at `Settings.Anim.DEFAULT` only. **A title is for show:** bounties and everything else count `enemy_name()`.

- **Colours ramp, they do not switch:** `_clock_color` and the counter's face are both shares of the whole, so retuning `Encounter.SECONDS` or the bag's cap retunes them.
- **`Palette.GOLD` is the unique item step:** the verdict's gold total is in the panel's own colour behind a coin.
- **The clock bar is `KillPips.width_for(slots)` less its border,** so pips and clock are one column to the pixel.
- **`Settings.animations` thins the view and never the fight:** at `LOW` a body throws one coin and one gem, a number neither pops nor drifts, and `Juice` shakes and freezes nothing; at `NONE` nothing is thrown or written and no body bursts -- but `_show_xp` still emits `xp_absorbed` (at once), and gold, finds and orbs had already gone out by their own signals. Anything new that is only for show asks the level; anything that carries an amount must not be behind it.
- **`Juice.hit_stop`: the one that ends last wins,** so a kill's freeze cannot cut short an elite or unique find's slow-motion (`STOP_UNIQUE`: a tenth of speed for 1.6 s, easing back over 0.8). The unique banner raises none of its own; the find already did.
- **`arm` clamps crit chance to `CRIT_CAP`;** crit damage is what a crit *adds* (50 = half again). An auto-swing only lands while an enemy is standing, so nothing banks through a walk-in.
- **Both hands are capped** (the user's, 2026-10-03): `arm` clamps `attack_speed` to `SWING_CAP` (10) last, after the tile's factor; the scene's clicks go through `click()`, an allowance that `advance` fills at `CLICK_CAP` (15) a second up to `CLICK_BURST` (3). A click past it is nothing -- no blow, no Knucklebone streak, no `clicks` tally -- though the scene still swings. **Tests and the camp call `hit()`, which is never capped**: tests click in loops without moving the clock.
- **A fight counts what it did for the achievements in `tally`, and nobody reads it but the main scene as the fight ends** (`Achievements.record` in `_on_combat_finished` and `_exit_tree`): `_count` for `clicks` (in `hit`, landed or not), `crits`, `crit_kills`, `bleed_kills` (`_wear_down(.., bleeding)`), `blows_taken` and `blocked` (in `_struck_by`, a dodge being neither), and `_best` for the longest `dry_streak` (blows without a crit) and `domino_streak` (kills by a first blow or by Dominoes' carry; a bleed kill breaks it). The camp's own fights keep a tally too and nobody reads it, which is why the camp counts nothing. **`unlocked`** is the uniques a body may carry (`Achievements.unlocked`, set by `_open_fight`); every `UniqueTable.roll` here passes it, and a fight nobody tells has none, so a hand-built one drops no unique, as it never did.
