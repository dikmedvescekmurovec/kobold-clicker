## Combat code (`Scenes/Combat/`)
A tile is taken by beating a lineup of enemies against a clock; a taken tile can be farmed endlessly with no clock. **Read `DESIGN.md` here before changing the HUD's layout, what drops show as, or the fight profiles** -- it holds the why.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `encounter.gd` (`Encounter`) | The rules, no nodes. `for_tile(cell, env, variant, chest)` rolls a lineup seeded from the cell; `farm(...)` is the same fight `endless`. `PROFILES` (keyed by `MapBuilder.area_variant`): `ORDINARY` is ten enemies in 30 s ending on an elite, `SETTLEMENT` fifteen in 60 s with an elite every fifth and a boss last, `CHEST` a lone `MIMIC`. `for_wall(cell)` is the ice wall: `WALL` profile, the lone `WALL_NAME` in 60 s on the `ice` backdrop, health `hp_of * WALL_HP * WALL_GROWTH ^ walls already fallen` (the dial and what each wall multiplies it by; `test_combat._test_the_ice_wall` pins bare hands and bare farmed rares out, and farmed rares with the whole Power tree at 1-8 clicks/s; `tests/balance_wall.gd` plays it out in kills). `arm(stats)` reads the player's stats once; `hit()` is a click, `advance(delta)` runs the clock, auto-swings and then wears the body down (`_wear_down`, Heatstroke and bleed), `stop()` ends a run, `kills()` is its score. Signals per enemy, hit, death and drop (`loot_dropped`, `gold_dropped`, `orb_dropped`, `xp_dropped`), then `won` / `lost`. `tier_in(slot)` is the tier both `KillPips` and `HealthBar` read. Health is `BASE_HP * HP_GROWTH ^ steps * hp_modifier`; gold is `base_gold(cell) * hp_modifier`, and `base_gold` is `gold_at_steps(distance)` so a price can be quoted in bodies with no cell to point at (`TownPrices`) |
| `fight_ledger.gd` (`FightLedger`) | What one fight has earned and the one bank-or-pouch rule (see `Scenes/CLAUDE.md`). No nodes; the main scene makes one per fight. `add_loot/gold/orb/xp`, `add_kill(enemy)` (a body for the bounty boards, `BountyBoard.count_kill`: counted and saved at once for a tile fight, pouched in `slain` and applied by `bank()` for a run), `autodiscarded`, `discard`, `room_left()`, `pending_xp()`, `bank()` (safe twice), `bank_kills` |
| `drops_view.gd` (`DropsView`) | A fight's finds as a grid of `ItemSlot`s on the bag's light `TextPanel`, with the orbs it is handed in a row under them, one find openable into an `ItemDetails` block; used by both the verdict panel and the loot popup. With `discardable`, emits `discarded(item)` and never edits its own list. `resized_contents` tells the owner to re-centre |
| `combat_actor.gd` (`CombatActor`) | An `AnimatedSprite2D` built from a sheet at runtime, for both sides. Scaled and stood up by its idle frames, so `position` is the middle of its feet |
| `combat_scene.tscn` / `combat_scene.gd` (`CombatScene`) | The view, a `CanvasLayer` (2) the main scene adds over the hidden map: `begin(fight, cell, ui_scale, variant)`. Top centre: place name and level, `KillPips`, clock. Bottom centre: enemy name and `HealthBar`. Bottom-right: loot counter (opens `_loot_panel`). Top-right: Terminate (farm runs). Signals out: `finished(won)`, `retry`, `loot_kept`, `loot_discarded`, `drop_discarded`, `gold_gained`, `orb_gained`, `xp_gained`, `xp_absorbed`. Set from outside: `autodiscard` (Callable), `bag_room` (-1 = unknown), `place`, `xp_target`. Backdrop is `Assets/Area/<env>_<variant>_<n>.png` via `backdrop_for` / `layout_for(cell)` |

`EnemyRoster` is in `Scenes/Enemies/`. Effects (`burst`, `hit_stop`, `shake`) come from `Juice` in `Scenes/UI/`.

## Rules and gotchas
- **An endless run has no clock and no loss.** `advance` leaves `time_left` alone while `endless`; a run ends only by `stop()` or `give_up()`. Anything reading `time_left`, `remaining()` or `enemies` must ask `endless` first, and the lineup grows, so `lineup.size()` is its only honest end.
- **`_append_enemy` is the one place an enemy joins a fight,** from `tier_for(position)`. Ask `EnemyRoster.tier_of`, never "is it the last one": a run's elites come round forever. A boss is only ever rolled as a settlement's last enemy, never in a run.
- **The lineup is seeded from the cell; loot is not.** `loot_rng` / `orb_rng` are unseeded, so re-fighting a tile drops something else. Tests seed them. `always_drop` exists for tests and screenshots only.
- **`CombatScene._on_loot_dropped` is the only listener to `Encounter.loot_dropped`** and the only place autodiscard is applied; it forks into `loot_kept` / `loot_discarded`. A discarded find appears nowhere and is counted once under the verdict.
- **A unique is rolled beside the gear, on `unique_rng`, and leaves through `loot_dropped` like any find.** `uniques_after` is `NO_UNIQUES` (-1) until the main scene sets it, so a hand-built fight drops none and every test that counts finds counts what it always did. Nothing promises one: past the wait it is `UniqueTable.chance_for` alone. Autodiscard never applies to one.
- **Everything a unique adds to a blow goes into one sum, `_unique_more(automatic)`, applied once** -- never a multiplier of its own: metronome (swings) and berserk (clicks) +200%, home and glass_edge +100%, last_gasp +200% under 5 s, knucklebone, ascetic +15% a bare socket, momentum +2% a kill to +100%, packmule +1% a bag piece. Outside it: the Gambler's Die (a random factor on the finished blow, its own `gamble_rng`), Giant Slayer / Giantsbane (the larger, never both), crits. **A new damage unique adds a line to that sum.** The tuning consts stand together above it, so a card's sentence and the fight agree.
- **`wear(effects)` before `arm(stats)` before `start()`.** `wear` sets `effects` and reshapes a tile fight's lineup for two home clauses -- Grazing (`_lead` commons on the front, seeded from the cell, `enemies` + 2) and Flush out (first elite slot swapped to the head, open land only) -- and `tier_for` reads `_lead` / `_elite_first`, so it still agrees with `tier_in`. `arm` reads what needs a number: `overcrit`, `heartwood` (+1 s per 100 health, `HEARTWOOD_MOST`), `block_chance` (Riposte, `RIPOSTE_CAP`), and `bare_sockets` / `bag_pieces`, which `Inventory.stats()` puts in. Setting `effects` by hand does all but the reshaping.
- **A wound is deepened, never stacked.** `arm` reads `bleed` (the mace's base stat); `_strike` sets `_bleed = maxf(_bleed, dealt * bleed / 100.0)` only where the body **survived** the blow, so a weapon swinging twice as fast cannot bleed twice as hard for free. It is cleared in `_advance_phase` beside `_struck`, so a body that gets back up (Restless dead) gets up whole.
- **`_wear_down(a_second, delta)` is the one path for damage nobody dealt** -- Heatstroke's share of the body's health and the wound's share of the blow, in that order, after `_swing_weapon`. `WAITING` only (a body walking in or going down takes none of it), and a death that way sets `_domino = false`, because it was nobody's blow.
- **Drop rate is the broad finder and is *added* to a narrow one where they meet** (`_kill`): `gold_find + drop_rate` for the purse, `orb_find + drop_rate` for `OrbTable.roll`, the way two global percents add. `gold_of` and `OrbTable.chance_for` are untouched, so what a body is worth -- and so a town's prices -- does not move.
- **`_kill()` is the one path to a death,** out of `_strike` because Heatstroke kills with no blow. Per-enemy flags `_struck` / `_crit_landed` / `_has_risen` are cleared in `_advance_phase`. **Restless dead rises in the slot it fell in** (`index` does not move, `enemy_coming` is re-sent), so the lineup and `KillPips` never change length; `kills()` is `index + _rose`. A home piece's second rule is asked with `_clause("<id>")`, which looks for `"<id>:<env>"`.
- **A worn unique's effect is one `if` in `Encounter`, beside the capstones',** and arrives in `effects` one entry a piece, so two of one ring are counted with `effects.count`. The first batch: `metronome` (a click lands nothing, the weapon's swing x3), `headsman` + `execute` (`EXECUTE_SHARE`, summed: 25%, 10%, 35%), `knucklebone` (clicks only; +2% a click within `KNUCKLE_WINDOW`, `KNUCKLE_MOST` a ring; a click that lands nothing still counts), `home:<env>` (x2 on that ground), `hourglass` (+1 s a kill, never past `seconds`, never for a BOSS, never `endless`), `tithe` (no `LootTable.roll` at all; purse x3, x5 for two). **Anything new that touches the clock must ask `endless` and must not be able to bank time.**
- **Orbs never enter `_drops`:** the counter, discard, `bag_room` and autodiscard know nothing about them. They roll on their own generator beside gear, not against it. `DropsView.fill(items, orbs)` only draws them, as a row of mouse-deaf `OrbSlot`s under the squares, and only the verdict hands them over -- so every `_result_drops.fill` passes `fight.orbs`.
- **The verdict reads top to bottom:** the word, one row of sums (coin, gem, skull -- bare numbers, no `+`; the kills are there for a tile fight too), then the finds and the orbs on the bag's `TextPanel`, then the autodiscard footnote and the way out, which `_on_finished` picks -- the word **Collect** (`_collect`) for a won tile and an ended run, the back arrow beside **Retry** (`_lost_row`) for a lost one. Retry emits `retry`, and the main scene's `_on_combat_retry` leaves through `_on_combat_finished(false, ..)` and comes back in through `_on_chart_pressed`, so a second go banks and opens like any other. Only a loss has a second line ("Out of time"); a win and a run say nothing under the word.
- **Everything in the HUD is placed by `_place_corners` every frame, not anchored:** it is scaled by `ui_scale`, which anchors know nothing about. Columns are centred, so a wider elite or boss bar grows evenly either side.
- **HUD words stand on the backdrop with no panel:** use `_hud_label` (bone with a dark outline) and `BAR_BORDER`, never a plain `Label`.
- **The nameplate is dressed once per enemy, in `_dress_nameplate` (from `_on_enemy_coming`), never in `_refresh`:** the name in `TIER_COLOUR` between `TIER_MARK`s (a bone skull for an elite, a gilded crown at 2x for a boss), an elite's `Encounter.enemy_title()` in front of its name, a boss's under it in the body font with the name at `BOSS_FONT` (32, Pixellari's one other clean size). A boss's plate pops in white (`_plate_pop`, which `_place_corners` multiplies into the panel's scale) at `Settings.Anim.DEFAULT` only. **A title is for show:** bounties and everything else count `enemy_name()`.
- **Tint the loot counter's styleboxes, not the Button:** `modulate` would take the icon and the count with it (`_tint_loot_button`, one copy per `UITheme.STATES`).
- **Colours ramp, they do not switch:** `_clock_color` and the counter's face are both shares of the whole, so retuning `Encounter.SECONDS` or the bag's cap retunes them.
- **`Palette.GOLD` is the unique item step:** the verdict's gold total is in the panel's own colour behind a coin.
- **The clock bar is `KillPips.WIDTH` less its border,** so pips and clock are one column to the pixel.
- **`Settings.animations` thins the view and never the fight:** at `LOW` a body throws one coin and one gem, a number neither pops nor drifts, and `Juice` shakes and freezes nothing; at `NONE` nothing is thrown or written and no body bursts -- but `_show_xp` still emits `xp_absorbed` (at once), and gold, finds and orbs had already gone out by their own signals. Anything new that is only for show asks the level; anything that carries an amount must not be behind it.
- **`Juice.hit_stop`: the latest call wins,** so a kill's freeze cannot cut short an elite find's slow-motion.
- **`arm` clamps crit chance to `CRIT_CAP`;** crit damage is what a crit *adds* (50 = half again). An auto-swing only lands while an enemy is standing, so nothing banks through a walk-in.
