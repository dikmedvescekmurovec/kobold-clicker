## Combat code (`Scenes/Combat/`)
A tile is taken by beating a lineup of enemies against a clock; a taken tile can be farmed endlessly with no clock, or **camped** -- that same run, played for the hours the game is shut on the best tile taken, at an eighth of what active play pays. **The dungeon** is the third kind: a clock and a lineup that never ends, in depths of fifteen floors, and a depth is won only by killing the Gollux at the foot of it. **Read `DESIGN.md` here before changing the HUD's layout, what drops show as, or the fight profiles** -- it holds the why.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `encounter.gd` (`Encounter`) | The rules, no nodes. `for_tile(cell, env, variant, chest)` rolls a lineup seeded from the cell; `farm(...)` is the same fight `endless`. `PROFILES` (keyed by `MapBuilder.area_variant`): `ORDINARY` is ten enemies in 30 s ending on an elite, `SETTLEMENT` fifteen in 60 s with an elite every fifth and a boss last, `CHEST` a lone `MIMIC`. `for_wall(cell)` is the ice wall: `WALL` profile, the lone `WALL_NAME` in 60 s on the `ice` backdrop, health `hp_of * WALL_HP` and no growth term of its own -- `hp_of(.., WALL_GROWTH)` already steps once per wall, and a wall's ring counts none of itself, so the second wall is `WALL_GROWTH` times the first on top of the walk between them (`WALL_HP` is the dial for how hard a wall is). `arm(stats)` reads the player's stats once; `hit()` is a click, `advance(delta)` runs the clock, auto-swings, lets the enemy strike (`_be_struck`) and then wears the body down (`_wear_down`, the bleed), `stop()` ends a run, `kills()` is its score. **Strikes:** while `strikes` (the main scene's, off in a hand-built fight) a standing enemy hits every `ATTACK_EVERY[tier]` (common 1 s, elite 2, boss 4) for `hit_of(enemy, cell)` = `HIT_SECONDS * HIT_GROWTH ^ steps * HIT_WALL_GROWTH ^ walls_inside(cell) * HIT_TIER[tier]` seconds of clock; `_struck_by(hit)` rolls `dodge_chance()`, else takes `taken(hit)` = the hit less armour's share (`rating / (rating + ARMOUR_K)`, the same share of any blow) less block, adds it to `wounds`, and emits `player_hit(taken, dodged, blocked)`. A landed hit of the player's gives back `min(time_on_hit, wounds)`. Signals per enemy, hit, death and drop (`loot_dropped`, `gold_dropped`, `orb_dropped`, `xp_dropped`), then `won` / `lost`. `tier_in(slot)` is the tier both `KillPips` and `HealthBar` read. Health is `BASE_HP * HP_GROWTH ^ steps * LAND_GROWTH ^ walls_inside(cell) * hp_modifier` -- **every wall behind a cell multiplies its bodies by `LAND_GROWTH` (10)**, while the walls themselves step by `WALL_GROWTH` (2.2), passed to `base_hp` / `hp_of` as `step`, and a blow (`hit_of`) grows on its own slower curve (`HIT_GROWTH` 1.1 a ring, `HIT_WALL_GROWTH` 1.5 a wall); which is what keeps the land a fallen wall opens from dying to one click; gold is `base_gold(cell) * hp_modifier` and does **not** step with it, and `base_gold` is `gold_at_steps(distance)` so a price can be quoted in bodies with no cell to point at (`TownPrices`) |
| `tile_mods.gd` (`TileMods`) | What land past the second wall does to its own fight, and what it pays. `MODS`: id -> `name`, `text` (dials in braces), `weight`, `farm`, `once`, `not_with`, and the dials `Encounter` reads by key -- `enemies` / `seconds` (added), `elite_every` (put in place, divided by the tier) / `elite_last`, `hp` / `hit` / `attack` (shares, added), `armor` / `dodge` / `block` / `time_on_hit` / `swing` / `walk_in` (factors), and the pay `drop_rate` / `item_rarity` / `gold_find` / `xp`. `for_cell(map_seed, cell, ring, wild)` is a pure function (none short of ring `FROM_WALLS` 2, then 1, 2, 3...: one more every ring of land, counted in the map's `wall_step`, so under the Ring of Walls each five-ring loop adds one; `wild` a wall sooner and one more). **Draws are put back: a modifier drawn twice is listed twice, and that is its tier** -- `total` adds and `factor` multiplies once a listing, so nothing else counts tiers; `value(id, key, tier)` for the one read by hand, `describe(id, tier)` for the tile panel's name / text / pay. `once` rows (Sparse, Barren, Gilded) never repeat. `farmable`, `total`, `factor`. `MapBuilder.mods_of` is its one caller in the game |
| `curses.gd` (`Curses`) | The twenty-two world curses as a table: id -> `name`, `skulls`, `text`, `reward`, and `stats` (what `Inventory.stats()` adds; `xp_more` is read by `Encounter.arm` alone). `skulls_of(taken)` is what a set of them costs and `fits(id, taken, budget)` whether one more is affordable and `allowed` (no cap on how many, only on skulls), `effect(id)` = `"curse:<id>"`, `known(saved)` drops ids this build has not got, `allowed(id, taken)` reads a row's `not_with` (Pacifist Hands and Berserker's World are no world together), `HOME_PREFIX` is how the Homeland's two lands reach a fight |
| `fight_ledger.gd` (`FightLedger`) | What one fight has earned and the one bank-or-pouch rule (see `Scenes/CLAUDE.md`). No nodes; the main scene makes one per fight. `add_loot/gold/orb/xp`, `add_kill(enemy)` (a body for the bounty boards, `BountyBoard.count_kill`: counted and saved at once for a tile fight, pouched in `slain` and applied by `bank()` for a run; either way `bounty_counted(enemy, have, need)` says what the accepted bounty stands at with it -- a run's off the board plus the pouch, `BountyBoard.takes` deciding, and a body past `need` said by neither), `autodiscarded`, `discard`, `room_left()`, `pending_xp()`, `bank()` (safe twice), `bank_kills` |
| `drops_view.gd` (`DropsView`) | A fight's finds as a grid of `ItemSlot`s on the bag's light `TextPanel`, with the orbs it is handed in a row under them, one find openable into an `ItemDetails` block; used by both the verdict panel and the loot popup. With `discardable`, emits `discarded(item)` and never edits its own list. `resized_contents` tells the owner to re-centre. Past `MAX_ROWS` (3.5) the grid scrolls by the wheel in a barless `ScrollContainer`, the half row being the hint; the box is sized by counting rows, since the grid is not laid out yet when `fill` runs |
| `camp.gd` (`Camp`) | The camp made for the hours the game was shut, all rules and no nodes. A camp is the tile's farm run played actively and cut to `IDLE_SHARE`: `rates(fight)` plays an armed endless `Encounter` clicked at `CLICK_RATE` (5 a second) for a sample (`SAMPLE_SECONDS` 120, on to `SAMPLE_KILLS` 5 bodies, `SAMPLE_MOST` 1800 either way, at `SAMPLE_STEP`) and hands back that share of what it earned a second in gold, experience and bodies -- **a full camp (`MAX_SECONDS`, 8 h) pays one hour of active play**. `make(cell, place, fight, at)` is the camp -- `SINCE`, `CELL`, `PLACE`, `MOST` and those three rates -- and `earned(camp, at)` is rate times seconds, clamped to its `MOST` and never negative, with `full` saying the cap is what stopped it. `cell_of`, `spell_time` |
| `camp_scene.gd` (`CampScene`) | The camp on screen, already paid: the tile's own backdrop at dusk (`NIGHT`) with **nobody on it**, and a centred panel -- the place, what a camp is, then Resting / Driven off / Gold / Experience from the `earned` it is handed, the cap's sentence where it bit, and **Break camp**. `begin(camp, earned, env, variant, ui_scale)`, signal `broke_camp`. A `CanvasLayer` on the fight's own layer 2, for `CombatScene`'s reason |
| `combat_actor.gd` (`CombatActor`) | An `AnimatedSprite2D` built from a sheet at runtime, for both sides. Scaled and stood up by its idle frames, so `position` is the middle of its feet |
| `combat_scene.tscn` / `combat_scene.gd` (`CombatScene`) | The view, a `CanvasLayer` (2) the main scene adds over the hidden map: `begin(fight, cell, ui_scale, variant)`. Top centre: place name and level, `KillPips`, clock. Bottom centre: enemy name and `HealthBar`. Bottom-right: loot counter (opens `_loot_panel`). Top-right: Terminate (a run: `stop()`; a tile fight: `give_up()`, a loss whose verdict says "Given up"). Signals out: `finished(won)`, `retry`, `loot_kept`, `loot_discarded`, `drop_discarded`, `gold_gained`, `orb_gained`, `xp_gained`, `xp_absorbed`. Set from outside: `autodiscard` (Callable), `bag_room` (-1 = unknown), `place`, `xp_target`. `hud_bottom()` is the bottom of the top-centre column, for anything the main scene stands over a fight and has to keep off it. Backdrop is `Assets/Area/<env>_<variant>_<n>.png` via `backdrop_for` / `layout_for(cell)` |

`EnemyRoster` is in `Scenes/Enemies/`. Effects (`burst`, `hit_stop`, `shake`) come from `Juice` in `Scenes/UI/`.

## Rules and gotchas
- **The dungeon (`Encounter.for_dungeon(won)`, `dungeon`, `first_floor`, `depth()`, `cleared()`) measures damage and nothing else,** and every rule of it is one `if dungeon` in `Encounter`: a floor's health is `BASE_HP * DUNGEON_GROWTH ^ floor * DUNGEON_TIER_HP[tier]` and nothing of the roster's sizes, a tile's modifiers or a world's curses (`_health_of`, which is handed the position when `_take_curses` sizes bodies again); `tier_for` counts in **floors** (`first_floor + position`), an elite every fifth and Gollux every fifteenth for ever; nothing strikes; `_kill` returns before anything is rolled, so it pays **nothing** -- no gear, purse, experience, unique, orb or Hourglass second; the clock runs only while a body stands (`advance`), so a floor's walk-in and death cost nothing; and the clock running out is `_finish(true)`, an end and not a loss. **A depth is fifteen floors and is won only when its Gollux has fallen:** `depth()` is `(first_floor + index) / enemies + 1`, so it moves as `index` passes him and never for a floor short of him, and `cleared()` is how many this descent has won. `for_dungeon(won)` begins at the top of the depth under the ones already won, with a whole clock, and a descent that kills Gollux goes on into the next depth on what is left of it. A click lands as it does anywhere (the user's ruling). **`endless` stays false:** anything that asks `endless` to mean "no clock" is right about the dungeon, and the four places that mean "the lineup grows" ask `dungeon` beside it (`_advance_phase`, `wear`, `KillPips.show_fight`, the view's Terminate). Reasoning and the turned-down shapes: `DESIGN.md`.
- **The view of it (`CombatScene`):** the backdrop is the cave's seven layers (`Assets/Area/cave/<n>.png`, **1 the nearest and 7 the flat dark; the pack's 0 is all of them put together, a preview, and is not exported** -- drawn as a layer it hid the rest and there was no parallax at all). `_build_cave` lays 7 down to 2 behind the fighters and `_build` adds **1 after them, so the nearest rock stands in front of the hero and the monsters**; each is a repeating region slid along while the next floor walks in (`_scroll_cave`, the farthest still and the nearest at `CAVE_SPEED`, the hero playing `walk`; none of it at `Settings.Anim.NONE`). **The fighters stand at `_ground()`, never at `GROUND`:** `CAVE_GROUND` down there, because the cave's floor lies lower than a backdrop's grass band and at `GROUND` everyone floated. The heading's gold half is "Depth n", rewritten in `_refresh`; no loot counter; Terminate is `stop()`; the verdict's word is the depth, its second line whether Gollux fell, no finds, and **Leave** in Collect's place. The main scene opens it with `_on_dungeon_pressed`, from the world's cave's Enter cave (`Scenes/CLAUDE.md`) -- no ledger, no board hears of a body, none of its kills are banked -- and `_bank_depths` writes `inventory.dungeon_depth` where the descent won any, and `inventory.dungeon_floors` (`first_floor + index`, the leaderboard's score) where it went further than ever, on the way out of it and out of the game.
- **A tile's modifiers reach a fight through `for_tile(.., mods)` / `farm(.., mods)` and nowhere else,** and a fight nobody tells has none. `_take_mods` shapes the fight right after the profile and before the lineup is rolled; what they do to the player's numbers and what they pay is the tail of `arm()`, added to the finders so `_kill` has no second path. **`farm` keeps only `TileMods.farmable`:** a run has no clock and is never struck, so a clock or strike modifier there would be pay for nothing. A chest and the wall carry none. Barren and Gilded are asked for by id in `_kill`.
- **A curse is one `if` on `Curses.effect(id) in effects`, like a worn unique's,** and the ones that shape the fight are taken on once in `wear` (`_take_curses`, `_cursed`): Iron Foes and the Bloodthirst add to the tile's own shares, Short Days cuts every clock with one, and a body's health is sized again through `_health_of` -- **only under a curse that moves health, so a fight built by hand keeps the health it was given**. `_health_of` is the one place a body's starting health is worked out; the ice wall answers there to `WALL_HP` and the Long Winter and to nothing else.
- **A finished chance is halved or doubled through `_lifted(rate, factor)`,** never by adding points: Lean Pickings hands every gear roll `_gear_rate()` (the purse, the orbs and the unique roll keep the full `drop_rate`), the Thick Fog hands the unique roll twice its chance. `_lean(item)` is the curse's pay -- a find falls +1 or +2 (`LEAN_PLUS`, `Item.ascend`) -- drawn on `loot_rng` **only under the curse**, so nobody else's loot stream moves; never the Broken Sword. **Hard Lessons is a "less" too:** `_kill` multiplies the finished experience by `LESSONS_XP` after every `xp_more` has been added, so a camp, which samples a real run, is taught as slowly.
- **A curse that is a unique's rule made a world's adds to the unique, in the unique's own place** (the user's ruling): Berserker's World is a line in `_unique_more` beside the Band's, the Glass World is `_glass()` -- how many things are spending the clock -- so the clock is `GLASS_CLOCK ^ n` and the damage `GLASS_MORE * n`, and Pacifist Hands stills the click where the Metronome does. **`swings()` is the one answer to whether the weapon swings on its own** (`_swing_weapon`). Pacifist Hands' swings are in `Inventory.stats()`, not here, so the character page sees them.
- **Raw Finds and the Homeland are two more hands on `_kill`:** `_raw(item)` strips a gear find to a bare common (never a unique, and before `_lean`, so a raw find can still fall +1); a fight abroad (`Curses.effect(HOMELAND)` without `HOME_PREFIX + env`) rolls no gear exactly as a Gilded tile does -- **but never a mimic or the wall, which are no land's** -- and at home `arm` adds `HOME_RARITY` and the unique roll is lifted `HOME_UNIQUES` times, multiplied with the Thick Fog's.
- **A Restless camp is still arithmetic:** `Camp.make` reads the curse off the fight's `effects`, doubles the two rates it pays and writes `MOST` (2 h) into the camp; `earned` clamps to its `MOST`, and one without fills at `MAX_SECONDS`.
- **`walk_in` is the fight's own** (`WALK_IN`, doubled in a Mire, cut by the gear's `spawn_speed` in `arm` -- to nothing at 100%, so `arm` works from the const and the view guards the division): tests that play an unmodified fight still step by the const.
- **A camp pays gold and experience and nothing else:** no gear, no orbs, no bodies for a bounty board and nothing on `inventory.kills` -- the gates those count towards are for going in with a sword in your hand. `Camp` never touches an `Inventory`; the main scene pays it at start-up.
- **A camp is never a second economy.** What it pays an hour is sampled off a real `Encounter.farm`, so every dial that moves what a tile is worth moves a camp with it and there is no figure here to keep in step. Nothing in `Camp` writes a number in gold.
- **The rates are worked out once and then it is arithmetic.** `make` samples at start-up, from the gear the save holds -- the gear the player shut the game in -- and `earned` is a sum over the hours since `Inventory.saved_at`.
- **An endless run has no clock and no loss.** `advance` leaves `time_left` alone while `endless`; a run ends only by `stop()` or `give_up()`. Anything reading `time_left`, `remaining()` or `enemies` must ask `endless` first, and the lineup grows, so `lineup.size()` is its only honest end.
- **`_append_enemy` is the one place an enemy joins a fight,** from `tier_for(position)`. Ask `EnemyRoster.tier_of`, never "is it the last one": a run's elites come round forever. A boss is only ever rolled as a settlement's last enemy, never in a run.
- **`even_loot` (dev, set by the main scene from `Settings.even_loot_on()`) replaces a body's gear and unique rolls** with one find at `EVEN_LOOT_CHANCE` (1/3), its rarity drawn evenly from common to unique (`_even_find`, `LootTable.roll(.., forced)`); a chest keeps its own rules, and the purse and the orb are rolled as ever.
- **The lineup is seeded from the cell; loot is not.** `loot_rng` / `orb_rng` are unseeded, so re-fighting a tile drops something else. Tests seed them. `always_drop` exists for tests and screenshots only.
- **A gear find that beat the chance rolls again, to `MOST_DROPS` (4) pieces off one body,** at the same chance and never `guaranteed` -- which is what stops a drop rate at `chance_for`'s ceiling looping for ever. **A certain roll never cascades:** the promised elite, a mimic's `MIMIC_ROLLS`, Trophy Hunter and `always_drop` each leave exactly one piece a roll. Lucky Wound still picks the better of two for the *first* find only, and the unique, the orb and the Magpie's purse-for-gear are separate single rolls.
- **`CombatScene._on_loot_dropped` is the only listener to `Encounter.loot_dropped`** and the only place autodiscard is applied; it forks into `loot_kept` / `loot_discarded`. A discarded find appears nowhere and is counted once under the verdict.
- **A unique is rolled beside the gear, on `unique_rng`, and leaves through `loot_dropped` like any find.** `uniques_after` is `NO_UNIQUES` (-1) until the main scene sets it, so a hand-built fight drops none and every test that counts finds counts what it always did. Nothing promises one but a chest: past the wait it is `UniqueTable.chance_for` alone. **The mimic is the one exception:** `_kill` tosses `MIMIC_UNIQUE` on `loot_rng`, and heads is one guaranteed unique (`UniqueTable.roll(..., guaranteed)`, no wait) and nothing else, tails is `MIMIC_ROLLS` (4) certain gear rolls; it never takes the ordinary unique roll. Autodiscard never applies to one.
- **Everything a unique adds to a blow goes into one sum, `_unique_more(automatic)`, applied once** -- never a multiplier of its own: metronome (swings), berserk (clicks), home, glass_edge, knucklebone, ascetic (a bare socket), packmule (a bag piece), patchwork, purist and brawler at their rank's numbers (`_dial`), last_gasp +200% in its rank's window, momentum +2% a kill to +100%. Outside it: the Gambler's Die (a random factor on the finished blow, its own `gamble_rng`), Giant Slayer, crits, and the Bulwark's answer at its rank's share. **A new damage unique adds a line to that sum.** Its ranked numbers are its own row's (`UniqueTable`) and the fixed ones stand together above it, so a card's sentence and the fight agree.
- **The Serpent's Eye is crit chance, not damage, so it stays out of that sum:** `_serpent` grows by its rank's step a Serpent's Eye (one on each doll counts twice, the user's ruling) for every blow that does not crit, is added to `crit_chance` under `CRIT_CAP` in `_strike`, and a crit empties it (at IV leaves `SERPENT_KEPT` of it). It lasts the whole fight, across bodies.
- **`wear(effects, ranks)` before `arm(stats)` before `start()`.** `wear` sets `effects` and `ranks` (unique id -> rank, `Achievements.ranks`; a unique it does not name is rank I, which is every hand-built fight) and takes the curses on. `arm` reads what needs a number: `overcrit` (its rank's times), `heartwood` (+1 s per `HEARTWOOD_ARMOUR` armour, its rank's most), the four defence stats (block and time on hit converted out of tenths by `LootTable.seconds_of`), and `bare_sockets` / `bag_pieces`, which `Inventory.stats()` puts in. Riposte is read in `_struck_by`: a blow block takes to nothing is answered by `_strike(true, true)`, at the Bulwark's rank's share. So are the Guard capstones: `afterimage` (a dodge wins back `min(AFTERIMAGE_SECONDS, wounds)`) and `shieldwall` (block doubled against an elite or boss, through `taken(hit, blocked)`); `second_wind` and the Worry Stone's `worry_stone` are read where the clock runs out in `advance`, once a fight each and the two adding up (`_saves_used`), never `endless`. `beginners_luck` is read in `arm` (the raw crit chance is `BEGINNERS_LUCK`, so the Overflowing Chalice gets nothing) and in `_strike` (the Serpent's Eye adds nothing to it). Setting `effects` by hand does all but take the curses on, at rank I.
- **A wound is deepened, never stacked.** `arm` reads `bleed` (the mace's base stat); `_strike` sets `_bleed = maxf(_bleed, dealt * bleed / 100.0)` only where the body **survived** the blow, so a weapon swinging twice as fast cannot bleed twice as hard for free. It is cleared in `_advance_phase` beside `_blows` -- unless the body died of it under a rank IV Butcher's Cleaver, which hands it to the next (`_bled_out`).
- **`_wear_down(a_second, delta)` is the one path for damage nobody dealt** -- the wound's share of the blow, after `_swing_weapon`, and the armour a rank IV Spiked Helm sends back (`_spikes_back`). `WAITING` only (a body walking in or going down takes none of it), and a death that way sets `_domino = false`, because it was nobody's blow.
- **Drop rate is the broad finder and is *added* to a narrow one where they meet** (`_kill`): `gold_find + drop_rate` for the purse, `orb_find + drop_rate` for `OrbTable.roll`, the way two global percents add. `gold_of` and `OrbTable.chance_for` are untouched, so what a body is worth -- and so a town's prices -- does not move.
- **`_kill(swung)` is the one path to a death,** out of `_strike` because a wound and Dominoes' carry kill with no blow; `swung` (the weapon's own swing felled it) is what a rank IV Metronome answers with the next swing ready. Per-enemy flags `_blows` / `_crit_kill` / `_walled` / `_bled_out` are cleared in `_advance_phase`; `_crit_kill` is whether the last blow critted, so Lucky Wound pays only where a crit felled the body (a wound's death clears it). `kills()` is `index`. **Dominoes carries `_carried`** -- its rank's share of a one-blow kill's overkill -- and deals it as the next body takes its stand (`_take_carried`); a body it fells is a one-blow kill and passes its own leftover on at the same share; an elite or a boss takes none of it (half at IV). Cleave's `_overkill`, and a rank IV Headsman's executed health, come off a body as it comes on and never fell one. Grazing, Flush out, Heatstroke, the Frozen clock, Giantsbane and Restless dead went with the Pilgrim's set (2026-09-29).
- **A worn unique's effect is one `if` in `Encounter`, beside the capstones',** and arrives in `effects` one entry a piece, so two of one ring are counted with `effects.count`. The first batch: `metronome` (a click lands nothing, the weapon's swing its rank's times), `headsman` + `execute` (`EXECUTE_SHARE`, a tenth, and the Headsman's rank's share, summed), `knucklebone` (clicks only; its rank's step a click within `KNUCKLE_WINDOW`, up to its rank's most a ring; a click that lands nothing still counts), `home:<env>` (its rank's times on that ground), `hourglass` (its rank's seconds a kill, never past `seconds`, never for a BOSS, never `endless`), `tithe` (no `LootTable.roll` at all; the purse its rank's times, two adding). **Anything new that touches the clock must ask `endless` and must not be able to bank time.**
- **A rank IV line is one `if` beside the rule it grows,** asking `_peak(id)` -- or `_home_peak()` on a home piece's own ground -- beside whether the piece is worn, its numbers consts after `MAGPIE_CHANCE` (`SERPENT_KEPT` ... `CHALICE_TWICE`). One that rolls draws only under its piece at IV (the Chalice on `crit_rng`, the second unique chance on `unique_rng`), so nobody else's rolls move; the clock ones (Rimeplate, Last Gasp, Hourglass, Heartwood) ask `endless` like the rest.
- **Nothing strikes a clock that is not there, or a wall.** `_be_struck` asks `strikes`, `endless` and `WALL_NAME` first; time on hit asks `endless`. A blow grows by `HIT_GROWTH` a ring and `HIT_WALL_GROWTH` a wall, far slower than health, because armour is a share and could not keep up with health's curve, and `HIT_TIER` against `ATTACK_EVERY` keeps every tier's seconds-a-second equal. `test_combat._test_enemy_strikes` pins the user's example (100 s against 90% armour and 10 s of block is nothing). `HIT_SECONDS`, `HIT_GROWTH`, `HIT_WALL_GROWTH`, `ARMOUR_K` and `DODGE_K` (50 each: 50 is half, 450 is 90%) are the dials.
- **The view answers `player_hit` in `_on_player_hit`:** the enemy plays `attack` (and `_on_enemy_hit` plays `hurt` only while it is not mid-attack, so a flinch never cuts a swing off), the clock flashes (`CLOCK_STRUCK`), and "-2.4s", "Dodge" or "Block" rises over the hero's head (`STRUCK_HEIGHT`, outlined). Everything that floats off a fight goes through `_float_text`.
- **Orbs never enter `_drops`:** the counter, discard, `bag_room` and autodiscard know nothing about them. They roll on their own generator beside gear, not against it. `DropsView.fill(items, orbs)` only draws them, as a row of mouse-deaf `OrbSlot`s under the squares, and only the verdict hands them over -- so every `_result_drops.fill` passes `fight.orbs`.
- **The verdict is a `UITheme.titled_panel` with no X** (the word on its green bar, `LightButton`s on its cream), popped in, counted up and revealed by `Juice` in `_on_finished`, celebrated unless lost, and left through `_leave` (Collect, the arrow, Retry and Escape all shrink it away first; `_on_back_pressed` itself is still immediate, which is what the tests call). `_centre_result` keeps it under `hud_bottom()` where the window has room.
- **The verdict reads top to bottom:** the word, one row of sums (coin, gem, skull -- bare numbers, no `+`; the kills are there for a tile fight too), then the finds and the orbs on the bag's `TextPanel`, then the autodiscard footnote and the way out, which `_on_finished` picks -- the word **Collect** (`_collect`) for a won tile and an ended run, the back arrow beside **Retry** (`_lost_row`) for a lost one. Retry emits `retry`, and the main scene's `_on_combat_retry` leaves through `_on_combat_finished(false, ..)` and comes back in through `_on_chart_pressed`, so a second go banks and opens like any other. Only a loss has a second line ("Out of time"); a win and a run say nothing under the word.
- **Everything in the HUD is placed by `_place_corners` every frame, not anchored:** it is scaled by `ui_scale`, which anchors know nothing about. Columns are centred, so a wider elite or boss bar grows evenly either side.
- **HUD words stand on the backdrop with no panel:** use `_hud_label` (bone with a dark outline) and `BAR_BORDER`, never a plain `Label`.
- **The nameplate is dressed once per enemy, in `_dress_nameplate` (from `_on_enemy_coming`), never in `_refresh`:** the name in `TIER_COLOUR` between `TIER_MARK`s (a bone skull for an elite, a gilded crown at 2x for a boss), an elite's `Encounter.enemy_title()` in front of its name, a boss's under it in the body font with the name at `BOSS_FONT` (32, Pixellari's one other clean size). A boss's plate pops in white (`_plate_pop`, which `_place_corners` multiplies into the panel's scale) at `Settings.Anim.DEFAULT` only. **A title is for show:** bounties and everything else count `enemy_name()`.
- **The loot popup (`_loot_panel`) is built as the verdict is** -- "Loot" on the green bar, the sums row (`_sums_row` / `_sum`, shared with the verdict, every sum shown even at 0 and kept current by `_refresh` while it is open), finds and orbs (`_fill_loot`, hidden while there are neither), a Close `LightButton` at the foot. **The counter is never disabled:** at 0 finds it still opens the sums.
- **Tint the loot counter's styleboxes, not the Button:** `modulate` would take the icon and the count with it (`_tint_loot_button`, one copy per `UITheme.STATES`).
- **Colours ramp, they do not switch:** `_clock_color` and the counter's face are both shares of the whole, so retuning `Encounter.SECONDS` or the bag's cap retunes them.
- **`Palette.GOLD` is the unique item step:** the verdict's gold total is in the panel's own colour behind a coin.
- **The clock bar is `KillPips.WIDTH` less its border,** so pips and clock are one column to the pixel.
- **`Settings.animations` thins the view and never the fight:** at `LOW` a body throws one coin and one gem, a number neither pops nor drifts, and `Juice` shakes and freezes nothing; at `NONE` nothing is thrown or written and no body bursts -- but `_show_xp` still emits `xp_absorbed` (at once), and gold, finds and orbs had already gone out by their own signals. Anything new that is only for show asks the level; anything that carries an amount must not be behind it.
- **`Juice.hit_stop`: the one that ends last wins,** so a kill's freeze cannot cut short an elite or unique find's slow-motion (`STOP_UNIQUE`: a tenth of speed for 1.6 s, easing back over 0.8). The unique banner raises none of its own; the find already did.
- **`arm` clamps crit chance to `CRIT_CAP`;** crit damage is what a crit *adds* (50 = half again). An auto-swing only lands while an enemy is standing, so nothing banks through a walk-in.
- **A fight counts what it did for the achievements in `tally`, and nobody reads it but the main scene as the fight ends** (`Achievements.record` in `_on_combat_finished` and `_exit_tree`): `_count` for `clicks` (in `hit`, landed or not), `crits`, `crit_kills`, `bleed_kills` (`_wear_down(.., bleeding)`), `blows_taken` and `blocked` (in `_struck_by`, a dodge being neither), and `_best` for the longest `dry_streak` (blows without a crit) and `domino_streak` (kills by a first blow or by Dominoes' carry; a bleed kill breaks it). The camp's own fights keep a tally too and nobody reads it, which is why the camp counts nothing. **`unlocked`** is the uniques a body may carry (`Achievements.unlocked`, set by `_open_fight`); every `UniqueTable.roll` here passes it, and a fight nobody tells has none, so a hand-built one drops no unique, as it never did.
