## Combat code (`Scenes/Combat/`)
A tile is taken by beating a lineup of enemies against a clock; a taken tile can be farmed endlessly with no clock. **Read `DESIGN.md` here before changing the HUD's layout, what drops show as, or the fight profiles** -- it holds the why.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `encounter.gd` (`Encounter`) | The rules, no nodes. `for_tile(cell, env, variant, chest)` rolls a lineup seeded from the cell; `farm(...)` is the same fight `endless`. `PROFILES` (keyed by `MapBuilder.area_variant`): `ORDINARY` is ten enemies in 30 s ending on an elite, `SETTLEMENT` fifteen in 60 s with an elite every fifth and a boss last, `CHEST` a lone `MIMIC`. `arm(stats)` reads the player's stats once; `hit()` is a click, `advance(delta)` runs the clock and auto-swings, `stop()` ends a run, `kills()` is its score. Signals per enemy, hit, death and drop (`loot_dropped`, `gold_dropped`, `orb_dropped`, `xp_dropped`), then `won` / `lost`. `tier_in(slot)` is the tier both `KillPips` and `HealthBar` read. Health is `BASE_HP * HP_GROWTH ^ steps * hp_modifier`; gold is `base_gold(cell) * hp_modifier`, and `base_gold` is `gold_at_steps(distance)` so a price can be quoted in bodies with no cell to point at (`TownPrices`) |
| `fight_ledger.gd` (`FightLedger`) | What one fight has earned and the one bank-or-pouch rule (see `Scenes/CLAUDE.md`). No nodes; the main scene makes one per fight. `add_loot/gold/orb/xp`, `add_kill(enemy)` (a body for the bounty boards, `BountyBoard.count_kill`: counted and saved at once for a tile fight, pouched in `slain` and applied by `bank()` for a run), `autodiscarded`, `discard`, `room_left()`, `pending_xp()`, `bank()` (safe twice), `bank_kills` |
| `drops_view.gd` (`DropsView`) | A fight's finds as a grid of `ItemSlot`s, one openable into an `ItemDetails` block; used by both the verdict panel and the loot popup. With `discardable`, emits `discarded(item)` and never edits its own list. `resized_contents` tells the owner to re-centre |
| `combat_actor.gd` (`CombatActor`) | An `AnimatedSprite2D` built from a sheet at runtime, for both sides. Scaled and stood up by its idle frames, so `position` is the middle of its feet |
| `combat_scene.tscn` / `combat_scene.gd` (`CombatScene`) | The view, a `CanvasLayer` (2) the main scene adds over the hidden map: `begin(fight, cell, ui_scale, variant)`. Top centre: place name and level, `KillPips`, clock. Bottom centre: enemy name and `HealthBar`. Bottom-right: loot counter (opens `_loot_panel`). Top-right: Terminate (farm runs). Signals out: `finished(won)`, `loot_kept`, `loot_discarded`, `drop_discarded`, `gold_gained`, `orb_gained`, `xp_gained`, `xp_absorbed`. Set from outside: `autodiscard` (Callable), `bag_room` (-1 = unknown), `place`, `xp_target`. Backdrop is `Assets/Area/<env>_<variant>_<n>.png` via `backdrop_for` / `layout_for(cell)` |

`EnemyRoster` is in `Scenes/Enemies/`. Effects (`burst`, `hit_stop`, `shake`) come from `Juice` in `Scenes/UI/`.

## Rules and gotchas
- **An endless run has no clock and no loss.** `advance` leaves `time_left` alone while `endless`; a run ends only by `stop()` or `give_up()`. Anything reading `time_left`, `remaining()` or `enemies` must ask `endless` first, and the lineup grows, so `lineup.size()` is its only honest end.
- **`_append_enemy` is the one place an enemy joins a fight,** from `tier_for(position)`. Ask `EnemyRoster.tier_of`, never "is it the last one": a run's elites come round forever. A boss is only ever rolled as a settlement's last enemy, never in a run.
- **The lineup is seeded from the cell; loot is not.** `loot_rng` / `orb_rng` are unseeded, so re-fighting a tile drops something else. Tests seed them. `always_drop` exists for tests and screenshots only.
- **`CombatScene._on_loot_dropped` is the only listener to `Encounter.loot_dropped`** and the only place autodiscard is applied; it forks into `loot_kept` / `loot_discarded`. A discarded find appears nowhere and is counted once under the verdict.
- **Orbs never enter `_drops`:** the counter, `DropsView`, discard, `bag_room` and autodiscard know nothing about them. They roll on their own generator beside gear, not against it.
- **Everything in the HUD is placed by `_place_corners` every frame, not anchored:** it is scaled by `ui_scale`, which anchors know nothing about. Columns are centred, so a wider elite or boss bar grows evenly either side.
- **HUD words stand on the backdrop with no panel:** use `_hud_label` (bone with a dark outline) and `BAR_BORDER`, never a plain `Label`.
- **Tint the loot counter's styleboxes, not the Button:** `modulate` would take the icon and the count with it (`_tint_loot_button`, one copy per `UITheme.STATES`).
- **Colours ramp, they do not switch:** `_clock_color` and the counter's face are both shares of the whole, so retuning `Encounter.SECONDS` or the bag's cap retunes them.
- **`Palette.GOLD` is the unique item step:** the verdict's gold total is in the panel's own colour behind a coin.
- **The clock bar is `KillPips.WIDTH` less its border,** so pips and clock are one column to the pixel.
- **`Settings.animations` thins the view and never the fight:** at `LOW` a body throws one coin and one gem, a number neither pops nor drifts, and `Juice` shakes and freezes nothing; at `NONE` nothing is thrown or written and no body bursts -- but `_show_xp` still emits `xp_absorbed` (at once), and gold, finds and orbs had already gone out by their own signals. Anything new that is only for show asks the level; anything that carries an amount must not be behind it.
- **`Juice.hit_stop`: the latest call wins,** so a kill's freeze cannot cut short an elite find's slow-motion.
- **`arm` clamps crit chance to `CRIT_CAP`;** crit damage is what a crit *adds* (50 = half again). An auto-swing only lands while an enemy is standing, so nothing banks through a walk-in.
