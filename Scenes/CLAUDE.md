# Main scene (`Scenes/main_scene.gd`) and rules that span several folders

**Read `Scenes/DESIGN.md` before changing how the bag, the character sheet, the orb tray, the tips or the balance are meant to work.** It holds the why; this file holds what must stay true.

## What the main scene owns
- **Exports:** `world_seed` / `map_seed` (0 = carry on from the save, else random; used seeds are printed), `map_origin` (even row), `zoom` (3; the mouse wheel steps it between `ZOOM_MIN` 1 and `ZOOM_MAX` 6 about the cursor, `_zoom_at`, on bare map outside a fight) and `ui_scale` (2), whole numbers only so a sprite pixel stays square, and `inventory_path` / `map_path`. Every counter in every settlement is the settings page's dev "Show all services" (`Settings.all_services`).
- **Start-up (`_ready`):** loads the inventory, then the map; either one unreadable stops everything (`_refuse_save`). Then builds or restores the map through `MapBuilder`, builds the UI, and saves the map once.
- **The UI is built in code**, so the `.tscn` stays untouched while the editor has it open. Shared builders are statics on `UITheme` (`titled_panel`, `body_of`, `button`, `icon_button`, `label`, `rule`, `vbox`, `clear`).
  - Right edge: the tile panel (`_panel`), a full-height column where a town's `TownPage` also stands, with the tile's name, level, one row per environment, and **at its foot** Chart / Move here / Farm / Enter town, each **shown only while it can be pressed** (`_update_buttons`, which re-places the column through `_place_panel` because its width may have changed). **Buttons stand at the foot of every right-hand column:** the tile panel's, a vendor's Restock and Buy, the smith's two, a bounty card's row.
  - Top-left: `CharacterPanel` on its own `CanvasLayer` 3, above a fight (layer 2). Under it the brown corner buttons (chest, the heirlooms' crown, star, scroll, cog, trophy) **in a column, the ones there are closed up** (`_place_corner`), which come and go together (`_show_corner`) -- a fight and a town take them away, **a page does not: the column moves to stand beside whichever page is up** (`BagPage.column_origin` and its `laid_out` for the bag, whose sheet comes and goes -- folded away, the column stands under the caret that brings it back; every other page's first child is its panel), so one press goes from page to page and a page's own button shuts it (`_toggle_left_page`, which also drops the selected tile outside a town, so nothing stays outlined behind the page); the scroll appears once a bounty board has been read, the cog -- the settings -- is there from the start, and the trophy -- the collection log -- once the first unique has been found (`inventory.uniques_found`), pulsing until pressed. **A press anywhere on the character panel opens `CharacterPage`:** the panel takes no mouse, so the press lands on `_character_button`, a see-through Button of the panel's size on the UI layer, shown and hidden by `_show_corner` with the rest -- which is what keeps it out of a fight's way.
  - **The heirlooms' crown** is a sixth corner button (stand-in mark `ui_icon_crown`), there while an heirloom is held, opening `heirloom_page` -- a second `BagPage`. **It is the one corner button a town leaves standing:** pressed there it swaps which bag page stands at the counter (`_counter_page`, which `_stand_at_counter` shops), and never closes the town. Both bag pages are wired to the town alike; a hidden one has nothing open and says nothing. `ItemCard.equipment` follows the page that is up, so Alt compares against the right doll.
  - Left edge: `BagPage`, `SkillsPage`, `BountyList`, `SettingsPage`, `CollectionPage` and `CharacterPage` (`Scenes/UI/`). The first three take the inventory, the save path and `ui_scale`; each saves what it changes itself, and offers `open()`, `layout()` and `closed`. Only one is ever up: `_open_left_page` / `_close_left_pages` are the one place that knows which pages there are.
  - Right edge, in the tile panel's place: `TownPage`, opened by the tile panel's **Enter town** (`MapBuilder.can_visit`). It takes the inventory and the save path the way the left-hand pages do, marks the town visited, fills its vendors' shelves, and saves what it sells and what its smith changes. It puts the bag in shop mode beside it (`_stand_at_counter`), the two hand each other the piece that is open (`offer_changed` / `selection_changed`), and `_close_town` is the one way back out -- the X on either page closes both.
  - **A bounty's promised piece** (`town_page.item_claimed`) is announced as a body's would be: `_is_new_unique` and `_announce_unique`, and nothing for any other piece.
  - **A bounty handed in raises the verdict's panel** (`town_page.bounty_paid` -> `_show_bounty_paid`): a `UITheme.titled_panel` with no X and "Bounty claimed" on its bar, the monster, gold and experience counting up behind their marks, and the orbs then the piece popping into a `DropsView` -- `Juice`'s, as the fight's verdict has -- on layer 3 behind a screen-wide catch, with **Collect** (or Escape) shrinking it away (`_leave_bounty_paid`, then `_close_bounty_paid`). Everything is already paid, so it only shows; the item card and tooltips are moved back over it.
  - `ChestPointer` (`Scenes/Map/`): `_sync_chest` (start-up, every arrival, `TownPage.chest_bought`) points it at `FortuneTeller.chest(inventory.fortunes)` while that chest still stands, and forgets the chest once it is charted. No chest is pointed at for nothing. **It is up for as long as the chest is**, under whatever the interface has open (`z_index` -1); a fight or a camp takes it away with the map.
  - The fortuneteller's two aimed spells, the scour and the road home: `TownPage.spell_aimed(reading, price, spot)` -> `_on_spell_aimed` closes the town and the tile panel, sets `map.aim_radius` (the scour's patch, or 0 -- one tile -- for the road home) and puts a panel up saying what to choose (`AIM_LINES`); `map.cell_aimed` -> `_on_cell_aimed` does the deed (`view.scour` / `view.jump_to`), and **only where it did something** charges, marks `FortuneTeller.ASKED + reading` in that town's drawer and saves the inventory (and the map, for a scour); `_end_aim` (the panel's X, Escape before anything else but a tip) costs nothing.
- **Super orbs (`_credit_walls`):** `inventory.credit_walls(view.walls_fallen())`, saved when it pays, asked at start-up before the interface is built (which pays a save from before there were heirlooms for the walls it has down) and after every `view.chart`. The tip `first_heirloom` is due while one is unspent, and says only that a fortuneteller now knows a way out.
- **Transcending is two steps.** `_on_transcend_pressed` (the fortuneteller's question answered yes; the price checked, never taken) closes everything and stands `TranscendPage` on the UI layer, the black screen where one heirloom is made and the super orbs are spent -- **in memory only**. Its `finished` -> `_transcend`, which is Reset with the inventory written over rather than deleted: `inventory.transcended().save` first, then the map deleted, then the reload, and `_resetting` keeps `_exit_tree` from writing the old world back. **Nothing may save the inventory while `_transcend_page` is up:** a game closed there has not transcended, which is what keeps closing it from being a way to make an heirloom and stay. Escape does nothing to the main scene while it is up.
- **Tile modifiers and curses:** `_mods_of(cell)` is `view.mods_of` under this world's Wild Tiles, handed to `Encounter.for_tile` / `farm` in all three places a fight is built (Chart, Farm, Set up camp); `_show_mods` writes them on the tile panel under the services in an `Accordion` (as the services are), each as its name with what it does and (in leaf) what it pays written under it, wrapped at `MOD_WIDTH` -- never in a tooltip -- **only for a seen tile**. `_sight()` loses its own ring under the Thick Fog, and `_cannot_camp()` answers for No Rest first. **A tile under the fog can be clicked** (`HexMap.can_pick`): `_on_tile_clicked` gets an empty `info`, calls it "Unknown land" without asking `name_of`, shows its level and no rows, and `_on_chart_pressed` reads the terrain from `view.env_at`, never from what is drawn.
- **Curses the main scene has a hand in:** at start-up, straight after the map is built, it sets `view.wall_step` for the Ring of Walls and runs `_settle_homeland()` -- the land under cell (0, 0) and one other seeded from the map, chosen once and saved with the inventory. `_cannot_camp()` answers for Berserker's World beside the Band. **No Second Chances:** `_on_combat_finished` ends on `_open_transcend(true)` when a tile's fight (never a run) was lost -- last, so the fight is banked and saved before the black screen, on which nothing may be -- and `_on_combat_retry` stops there. `_open_transcend(lost)` is the one way onto that screen; the fortuneteller's `_on_transcend_pressed` checks the price and calls it with false.
- **The dungeon (`_on_dungeon_pressed`, `_on_dungeon_finished`, `_bank_depths`):** a seventh corner button, the pack's skull, between the scroll and the cog, which comes on with the bag (it is gear that it measures) and says the depth the next descent begins in. It opens `Encounter.for_dungeon(inventory.dungeon_depth)` in `CombatScene` the way `_open_fight` opens a fight but with **no ledger and nothing connected but `finished`**: it pays nothing, no board hears of a body, and none of its kills are banked. `_bank_depths` adds the depths the descent won -- a Gollux each -- and saves, on the way out of it and from `_exit_tree`, so quitting under a dead Gollux cannot cost him. **`dungeon_depth` only rises and is carried through every transcension** (the user's ruling).
- **Camps (`_on_camp_pressed`, `_open_camp`, `_break_camp`):** the tile panel's **Set up camp**, beside Farm and on the same ground (`MapBuilder.can_farm`), arms a farm run exactly as `_open_fight` does, hands it to `Camp.make` and saves `inventory.camp`. `_open_camp` stands `CampScene` over the hidden map the way a fight stands there; `_break_camp` pays `Camp.earned` into the purse and the experience, strikes the camp and brings the map back. **A camp is somewhere the player is,** so `_ready` opens one last of all where the save has one -- the hours the game was shut are the hours it paid for -- and Escape does nothing while it is up, as on the transcension's black screen. `_cannot_camp()` is why the button is greyed: a weapon that does not swing on its own, or the Berserker's Band, would rest all night for nothing.
- **Fights:** `_open_fight` arms an `Encounter` from `inventory.stats()`, makes a `FightLedger`, hides the map and every left-hand Control, and wires `CombatScene`'s signals to the ledger. `_on_combat_finished` banks, charts the tile if it was won, and brings the map back.
- **The banner (`_raise_banner`, `_banner`, `_close_banner`, `_place_banner`):** one cream panel on the character's layer 3, centred under the fight's own top column (`CombatScene.hud_bottom`), a square beside a column of lines at `BANNER_WIDTH` -- half again the card's, because the banner has room across the screen and none down it. Two things raise it. **A unique new to the collection log (`_announce_unique`, `_is_new_unique`):** the piece as an `ItemSlot` beside its name, rarity line and rule, raised from `_on_loot_dropped` **before** `ledger.add_loot`, which is the last moment `uniques_found` can still answer "is this new?"; `ledger.drops` is asked as well, because a run's pouch has not reached the log yet; it alone gets the `Juice.hit_stop`. **A bounty filled (`_announce_bounty`):** the monster's picture (`BountyList.portrait_box`) beside its name, the count and "Claim it at <town>." (`bounty_page.town_name` off `BountyBoard.active_spot`). Either is **up for `BANNER_HOLD` (5 s) whatever happens**, and then `_on_banner_held` decides: a player who clicked through them has it taken away, one who sat still gets an X and keeps it until that or the next left press (`_input`, which never marks the event handled, so the swing still lands). The hold timer holds the panel through a `weakref`, since a banner put down early is freed before it fires. `_close_banner` is the one way down, and `_on_combat_finished` calls it so it is never left over a verdict. Placement re-runs on the panel's `resized`, and accounts for `pivot_offset`, about which a scaled Control is drawn.
- **A counted bounty kill is the toast (`_toast_bounty`, `_place_toast`, `_toast`):** "+1" beside the monster's picture on a small cream panel under the same column (under the banner while one is up), popped, risen `TOAST_RISE` and gone in `TOAST_TIME`; the latest wins, none at `Anim.NONE` (the journal carries the count). An elite's or a boss's stands taller, its picture framed and its count at `BOSS_FONT` behind the nameplate's mark (`BOUNTY_TIER`: the frame and the cream-half colour a tier wears). `_on_bounty_counted` picks: the kill that fills the bounty is the banner and no toast -- so an elite bounty, filled by its one kill, is the banner alone -- and every kill before it the toast; the banner puts any toast down.
- **Tips (`TIPS`, `_check_tips`):** one-time pop-ups, one at a time, on layer 3. Seen ids live in `inventory.tips`, which also drives which corner buttons exist and whether they still pulse (`_flash`, `opened_bag` / `opened_heirlooms`). The star and the trophy pulse on state instead (`_pulse`): the star while a rank can be bought, the trophy while `inventory.uniques_new` holds a find not yet hovered in the log. A tip over a fight pauses the fight's processing. Each is checked where the thing it is about becomes true: `_open_fight` and `_on_combat_finished` for the fighting ones, `_on_player_arrived` for `first_town` (which is how a won settlement fight reaches it too) and `_on_town_pressed` for `first_bounty`, since a town always opens on its board. `_check_tips` puts the corner buttons back, so it asks about the fight; `_show_corner` itself knows a town is open.
- **Escape (`ui_cancel`, `_unhandled_input`):** closes a tip if one is up, otherwise the pages, the town and the tile panel in one press. A fight gets the key first and answers for itself (`CombatScene._unhandled_input`: loot popup, then Back under a verdict, then Terminate on a run; a tile fight ignores it, and is given up only by its Terminate button). So does the bag (`BagPage._unhandled_input`): Escape over its question is Cancel, over a held orb puts it down, over a selected piece clears the selection -- each time the page stays up, and only a bare bag lets the key through to close it.
- **Reset:** the settings page's **Reset save**, in every build, asks first and then emits `reset_pressed`; `_on_reset_pressed` deletes both saves and reloads, and `_resetting` stops `_exit_tree` writing them back. The settings file is not a save and stays.
- **Dev skip fight:** a debug-build-only button under Chart calls `view.chart` on the selected tile with no fight, so no loot, kills or tips come of it.
- **The play clock:** `_process` adds every frame's delta to `inventory.play_seconds`, which is written whenever anything else writes the inventory -- so a quit loses the seconds since the last save. The settings page is the only thing that reads it.

## Rules and gotchas
- **`Settings` (`Scenes/settings.gd`) is static and read where it matters, never passed around:** `Juice.shake` / `hit_stop` and `CombatScene`'s `_show_*` ask `Settings.animations`, `ItemDetails.fill` asks `Settings.item_details`, and the fight's sound plays on `Settings.SFX_BUS` (the two buses are made at runtime by `apply_audio`; nothing plays on `Music` yet). **`Settings.path` is empty unless the main scene is on the player's own save,** so tests and screenshot scripts see the defaults and write nothing -- a test that moves a static puts it back.
- **A save that cannot be read is refused, never overwritten.** `_refuse_save` runs before any UI exists, so nothing can write; `_save_blocked` also stops `_save_map` and `_exit_tree`. Only a *missing* file means a fresh start.
- **The map is written in four places:** the end of `_ready`, every arrival (`_on_player_arrived`), a paid scour (`_on_cell_aimed`, the one other place the map's state changes) and `_exit_tree`.
- **A non-zero seed in the scene wins over a save of another world**, and so does a changed `map_origin`. Every test and screenshot script pins a seed, so every one of them **must redirect `map_path` and `inventory_path`** or it replaces the player's saves.
- **Bank or pouch is `FightLedger`'s rule and nobody else's.** A tile fight banks and saves each find, purse, orb and experience as it lands; a farm run holds them until `bank()`. **A body counted against a bounty follows it too:** `_open_fight` connects the fight's `enemy_died` to `_on_enemy_died`, which hands the enemy's name to `ledger.add_kill`, and the ledger decides whether the boards hear about it now or when the run banks. Nothing in the interface counts a kill: what the screen hears is the ledger's `bounty_counted(enemy, have, need)` (`_on_bounty_counted`), said as a tile fight writes the board and, for a run, as what the pouched body will count as at `bank` -- a preview, never wrong, since both ways out of a run bank. Both ways out of a run bank (`_on_combat_finished`, `_exit_tree`), and `bank()` is safe to call twice. Gold, orbs and experience can never be refused: the bag's cap and autodiscard apply to gear only.
- **Autodiscard is applied in exactly one place, `CombatScene._on_loot_dropped`.** The main scene listens to `loot_kept` / `loot_discarded`, never to `Encounter.loot_dropped`, so the counter, the pouch and the bag cannot disagree.
- **No Control may sit in the top-left corner during a fight:** it would take the mouse before the fight saw it and eat the player's swings. `_open_fight` closes the pages and hides the corner buttons; `CharacterPanel` ignores the mouse.
- **No orb drops before the player's 50th kill** (`OrbTable.FIRST_ORB_KILLS`): `_open_fight` passes what is left to `Encounter.orbs_after`, and `FightLedger.bank_kills` adds a fight's kills when it ends or the game closes.
- **No unique drops before the player's 100th kill** (`UniqueTable.FIRST_UNIQUE_KILLS`, passed as `Encounter.uniques_after`), and after it pure chance: **no unique is ever promised, except by a chest** -- the mimic's coin toss (`Encounter.MIMIC_UNIQUE`) pays one unique, or else `MIMIC_ROLLS` certain pieces of gear, whatever the kill count.
- **The player's first piece of gear is a Broken Sword** (`LootTable.FIRST_DROP`: 1 Damage and nothing else, always common and level 1, orbs work on it; weight 0 so nothing else deals one; `Encounter.first_sword` from `inventory.first_sword_taken`, which the ledger sets on any drop, autodiscarded or not, by the bank-or-pouch rule; a save older than version 13 reads as taken).
- **An elite is promised a drop only while the Broken Sword has not dropped** (`guarantee_elite = first_sword`), so the promise is always the sword; once anything has dropped there is no promise at all.
- **A fight reads the offensive stats, `bleed`, the defence, the three finders and the skill-only `orb_find`:** `damage`, `crit_chance`, `crit_damage`, `attack_speed`, `bleed`, `armor`, `dodge`, `block`, `time_on_hit`, `drop_rate`, `item_rarity`, `gold_find`, `orb_find`, and `spawn_speed` (a share off the walk-in, capped at 100, where the next enemy is simply there), all read in `Encounter.arm()`. The attributes are the one thing gear carries that nothing reads. **Drop rate is the broad finder:** it lifts the gear roll and the unique roll, and in `_kill` it is **added** to `gold_find` for the purse and to `orb_find` for the orb roll, the way two global percents add. `gold_of` and `OrbTable.chance_for` themselves are untouched, so town prices -- quoted in bodies, with no player in them -- do not move. **`move_speed` is read by no fight either:** `PlayerToken.move_speed` (set in `_ready`) asks the worn set as each map walk sets off. **`sight` is the live stat no fight reads:** `main_scene._sight()` asks the worn set for it where a won tile is charted (`_on_combat_finished`, and the dev skip-fight) and nowhere else. Capstone skills and worn uniques arrive together as `Encounter.effects` (`Inventory.effects()`).
- **The enemies strike the clock, and the main scene is what turns it on** (`fight.strikes = true` in `_open_fight`; a hand-built fight is never struck, so every test that times a clock to the second still does). Armour takes a share of each blow, block (kept in **tenths of a second**, `LootTable.SECONDS_STATS`) a flat amount after it, dodge now and then all of it, and time on hit wins back what the blows took and never more. A farm run, a camp and the ice wall are never struck. Three uniques put defence to a second use: armour feeds damage under the Spiked Helm, armour buys clock under the Heartwood Plate, a blow blocked whole is answered with a swing under the Bulwark. Health, energy shield, regen, leech and the resistances are gone, with the hat, robe, slippers and Sapphire Amulet built on them.
- **Monsters outgrow gear on purpose.** `HP_GROWTH` is per hex step, `WALL_GROWTH` is per ice wall behind the cell (`Encounter.walls_inside`, so the land a fallen wall opens is a frontier again and not a walkover), `LEVEL_GROWTH` is per level, and levels come in widening bands. `test_combat._test_a_won_fight` pins the ends, two rings of them (first ring beatable bare-handed; the last ring **inside** the wall falls to a plain set of commons, the wall being the check there and not the land, and a better material still wants fewer clicks; the first ring **past** the wall is beyond that same farmed set of rares on its own and comes under 8 clicks a second -- and over 1 -- once the Power tree the wall already asked for is behind it). The farmed reference is `_typical_farmed`, the median of `FARMED_SETS` seeded sets, never one set. Dials: `HP_GROWTH`, `WALL_GROWTH`, `LEVEL_GROWTH`, `LEVEL_FLAT`, `LootTable.TIER_POWER`, a kind's own `power`, `Encounter.SECONDS`.
- **Selecting a bag item draws nothing of what it would replace: the doll stays, and the worn piece is the hover card's second card under Alt** (`ItemCard.worn_for`, the first socket of `Equipment.sockets_for` that `displaced_by` finds anything in). Equip targets the emptiest socket the piece fits (`sockets_for(item)[BagPage._socket_pick]`); for a ring a **Swap** beside Equip in `_actions` turns it to the other finger, and every new selection starts on the emptiest again. Its tooltip names everything the press takes off. **Hide** (the caret in the doll's top-right corner) folds the doll away for the session (`_doll_hidden`, not saved) and leaves **Show** (the caret turned round, `_show_button`) against the bag's edge. No block anywhere shows deltas.
- **A bag square takes no mouse input** (`MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE`): the `ScrollContainer` must see every press to tell a drag from a click, so the clicked square is worked out from the cursor position.
- **Tip copy:** a short title-case title, then two or three plain sentences in the game's voice: what happened, then what to do, naming a button by its picture and corner. No system words ("pouch", "autodiscard"), no dashes, nothing Pixellari may not draw.
- **A town is one thing on screen, not two pages.** `TownPage` on the right and the bag in shop mode on the left open and close together, and the town's own cell (`_town_cell`) is what the bag is pointed at -- never `map.selected_cell`, which is still clickable behind the page. `_close_town` runs from `_open_fight` as well, so a town goes away with the left pages.
- **The one hand that moves a held piece's level and base stats is the fortress smith** (`Blacksmith`, reached from `TownPage`'s smith tab), and it works on what the **bag page** has open, carried or worn and never one off a shelf, which is the crafting rule the orb tray obeys. Its cap is the ground's own ceiling, so no counter walks gear past the frontier.
- **Money has one place it is made, `TownPrices`, and one unit, bodies.** Anything that pays or charges asks there; nothing outside it writes a figure in gold. The purse it is quoted against is `Encounter.gold_at_steps`, which `base_gold` is also built on, so the tile's payout and the shop's prices cannot drift apart.
- **A town's counters are derived and a town's doings are written down.** `TownServices.services_for` is a pure function of tier, spot and world seed and may be asked twice; anything rolled -- a vendor's shelf (`VendorStock`) and a board's postings (`BountyBoard`) -- goes in `TownState` inside `inventory.towns`, keyed by **world spot** as `"x,y"`, because a purchase moves the purse, the bag and the town together and one save is one write.
- **Where a monster lives is only ever somewhere the player has seen, and only once a fortuneteller has been paid to say** (`BountyBoard.located`; a missing unique's ground likewise, `FortuneTeller.peeked`). A bounty row's swatches are the tile panel's own (`HexTileset.env_icon`) and its "Nearest" is `MapBuilder.nearest_env`, which never offers a cell under the fog, never a settlement, and measures from where the player stands rather than from the town that posted the work. A page's Show says which cell and nothing more: the main scene closes the pages, selects it and moves the camera, and the tile panel's Move here / Farm / Chart do the rest.
- **Gear thrown away pays `Inventory.salvage(item)` in all four places it can be thrown away** -- nothing unless the Rag and Bone Sack is worn: `_on_loot_autodiscarded` and `_on_drop_discarded` through `_pay_salvage` and `ledger.add_gold` (so a run pouches it), `BagPage._on_discard_pressed` and `_on_clear_level_pressed` straight to the purse. A full bag never destroys a piece: past `CAPACITY` the player is overencumbered instead.
- **`_open_fight` calls `fight.wear(inventory.effects())` before `fight.arm(inventory.stats())`:** `arm` reads some effects, and `wear` reshapes the lineup before the view is built.
- **Nothing calls `Inventory.add` where the bag may be full** except a drop. A drop past `CAPACITY` is kept and leaves the player **overencumbered** (`Inventory.encumbered()`): `_update_buttons` greys Chart and Farm and `_cannot_camp` answers for Set up camp (`ENCUMBERED_TIP`), their handlers refuse too (Retry and a walk-then-chart come through `_on_chart_pressed`), the token walks at `Inventory.ENCUMBERED_SPEED`, and `_process` re-runs `_update_buttons` the moment the bag crosses the cap, so a discard or a sale anywhere brings the buttons back. A purchase is refused instead, greyed with the reason, because it would overencumber the player at a counter. **Equip is greyed the same way** where a two-hander would hand two pieces back into a bag with room for one (`Inventory.can_equip`).
- **A growing quantity is a whole-valued `float` and is written only through `BigNumber.format`** (`Scenes/big_number.gd`): gold, every price and reward, monster health and damage. Round with `roundf` / `maxf`, never `roundi` / `maxi`, which overflow at 9.2e18; print with `%s`, never `%d`. Below a million it reads as the whole number, from there as `1.23e6`. Levels, kills, counts, percents, experience and modifier values stay `int`. `round()` / `max()` return a Variant and will not parse under `:=`.
