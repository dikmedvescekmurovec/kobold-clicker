# Main scene (`Scenes/main_scene.gd`) and rules that span several folders

**Read `Scenes/DESIGN.md` before changing how the bag, the character sheet, the orb tray, the tips or the balance are meant to work.** It holds the why; this file holds what must stay true.

## What the main scene owns
- **Exports:** `world_seed` / `map_seed` (0 = carry on from the save, else random; used seeds are printed), `map_origin` (even row), `zoom` (3) and `ui_scale` (2), whole numbers only so a sprite pixel stays square, and `inventory_path` / `map_path`.
- **Start-up (`_ready`):** loads the inventory, then the map; either one unreadable stops everything (`_refuse_save`). Then builds or restores the map through `MapBuilder`, builds the UI, and saves the map once.
- **The UI is built in code**, so the `.tscn` stays untouched while the editor has it open. Shared builders are statics on `UITheme` (`titled_panel`, `body_of`, `button`, `icon_button`, `label`, `rule`, `vbox`, `clear`).
  - Right edge: the tile panel (`_panel`), a full-height column where a town's `TownPage` also stands, with the tile's name, level, one row per environment, and **at its foot** Chart / Move here / Farm / Enter town, each **shown only while it can be pressed** (`_update_buttons`, which re-places the column through `_place_panel` because its width may have changed). **Buttons stand at the foot of every right-hand column:** the tile panel's, a vendor's Restock and Buy, the smith's two, a bounty card's row.
  - Top-left: `CharacterPanel` on its own `CanvasLayer` 3, above a fight (layer 2). Under it the five brown corner buttons (chest, star, scroll, cog, trophy) **in a column, the ones there are closed up** (`_place_corner`), which come and go together (`_show_corner`) -- a fight and a town take them away, **a page does not: the column moves to stand beside whichever page is up** (`BagPage.right_edge` and its `laid_out` for the bag, whose sheet comes and goes; every other page's first child is its panel), so one press goes from page to page and a page's own button shuts it (`_toggle_left_page`); the scroll appears once a bounty board has been read, the cog -- the settings -- is there from the start, and the trophy -- the collection log -- once the first unique has been found (`inventory.uniques_found`), pulsing until pressed. **A press anywhere on the character panel opens `CharacterPage`:** the panel takes no mouse, so the press lands on `_character_button`, a see-through Button of the panel's size on the UI layer, shown and hidden by `_show_corner` with the rest -- which is what keeps it out of a fight's way.
  - Left edge: `BagPage`, `SkillsPage`, `BountyList`, `SettingsPage`, `CollectionPage` and `CharacterPage` (`Scenes/UI/`). The first three take the inventory, the save path and `ui_scale`; each saves what it changes itself, and offers `open()`, `layout()` and `closed`. Only one is ever up: `_open_left_page` / `_close_left_pages` are the one place that knows which pages there are.
  - Right edge, in the tile panel's place: `TownPage`, opened by the tile panel's **Enter town** (`MapBuilder.can_visit`). It takes the inventory and the save path the way the left-hand pages do, marks the town visited, fills its vendors' shelves, and saves what it sells and what its smith changes. It puts the bag in shop mode beside it (`_stand_at_counter`), the two hand each other the piece that is open (`offer_changed` / `selection_changed`), and `_close_town` is the one way back out -- the X on either page closes both.
  - `ChestPointer` (`Scenes/Map/`): `_sync_chest` (start-up, every arrival, `TownPage.chest_bought`) points it at `FortuneTeller.chest(inventory.fortunes)` while that chest still stands, and forgets the chest once it is charted. No chest is pointed at for nothing. **It stands down while anything else is up** (`covered`: the tile panel, a town, a left-hand page, a tip, the scour).
  - The fortuneteller's scour: `TownPage.scour_pressed(price)` -> `_on_scour_pressed` closes the town and the tile panel, sets `map.aim_radius` and puts a small panel up; `map.cell_aimed` -> `_on_cell_aimed` charges, marks `fortunes.scoured`, and saves the inventory **and the map**; `_end_scour` (the panel's X, Escape before anything else but a tip) costs nothing.
- **Fights:** `_open_fight` arms an `Encounter` from `inventory.stats()`, makes a `FightLedger`, hides the map and every left-hand Control, and wires `CombatScene`'s signals to the ledger. `_on_combat_finished` banks, charts the tile if it was won, and brings the map back.
- **Tips (`TIPS`, `_check_tips`):** one-time pop-ups, one at a time, on layer 3. Seen ids live in `inventory.tips`, which also drives which corner buttons exist and whether they still pulse (`_flash`, `opened_bag` / `opened_skills`). A tip over a fight pauses the fight's processing. Each is checked where the thing it is about becomes true: `_open_fight` and `_on_combat_finished` for the fighting ones, `_on_player_arrived` for `first_town` (which is how a won settlement fight reaches it too) and `_on_town_pressed` for `first_bounty`, since a town always opens on its board. `_check_tips` puts the corner buttons back, so it asks about the fight; `_show_corner` itself knows a town is open.
- **Escape (`ui_cancel`, `_unhandled_input`):** closes a tip if one is up, otherwise the pages, the town and the tile panel in one press. A fight gets the key first and answers for itself (`CombatScene._unhandled_input`: loot popup, then Back under a verdict, then Terminate on a run; a tile fight ignores it). So does the bag's question (`BagPage._unhandled_input`): Escape there is Cancel, and the page under it stays up.
- **Reset:** the settings page's **Reset save**, in every build, asks first and then emits `reset_pressed`; `_on_reset_pressed` deletes both saves and reloads, and `_resetting` stops `_exit_tree` writing them back. The settings file is not a save and stays.
- **Dev skip fight:** a debug-build-only button under Chart calls `view.chart` on the selected tile with no fight, so no loot, kills or tips come of it.

## Rules and gotchas
- **`Settings` (`Scenes/settings.gd`) is static and read where it matters, never passed around:** `Juice.shake` / `hit_stop` and `CombatScene`'s `_show_*` ask `Settings.animations`, `ItemDetails.fill` asks `Settings.item_details`, and the fight's sound plays on `Settings.SFX_BUS` (the two buses are made at runtime by `apply_audio`; nothing plays on `Music` yet). **`Settings.path` is empty unless the main scene is on the player's own save,** so tests and screenshot scripts see the defaults and write nothing -- a test that moves a static puts it back.
- **A save that cannot be read is refused, never overwritten.** `_refuse_save` runs before any UI exists, so nothing can write; `_save_blocked` also stops `_save_map` and `_exit_tree`. Only a *missing* file means a fresh start.
- **The map is written in four places:** the end of `_ready`, every arrival (`_on_player_arrived`), a paid scour (`_on_cell_aimed`, the one other place the map's state changes) and `_exit_tree`.
- **A non-zero seed in the scene wins over a save of another world**, and so does a changed `map_origin`. Every test and screenshot script pins a seed, so every one of them **must redirect `map_path` and `inventory_path`** or it replaces the player's saves.
- **Bank or pouch is `FightLedger`'s rule and nobody else's.** A tile fight banks and saves each find, purse, orb and experience as it lands; a farm run holds them until `bank()`. **A body counted against a bounty follows it too:** `_open_fight` connects the fight's `enemy_died` to `_on_enemy_died`, which hands the enemy's name to `ledger.add_kill`, and the ledger decides whether the boards hear about it now or when the run banks. Nothing in the interface counts a kill. Both ways out of a run bank (`_on_combat_finished`, `_exit_tree`), and `bank()` is safe to call twice. Gold, orbs and experience can never be refused: the bag's cap and autodiscard apply to gear only.
- **Autodiscard is applied in exactly one place, `CombatScene._on_loot_dropped`.** The main scene listens to `loot_kept` / `loot_discarded`, never to `Encounter.loot_dropped`, so the counter, the pouch and the bag cannot disagree.
- **No Control may sit in the top-left corner during a fight:** it would take the mouse before the fight saw it and eat the player's swings. `_open_fight` closes the pages and hides the corner buttons; `CharacterPanel` ignores the mouse.
- **No orb drops before the player's 50th kill** (`OrbTable.FIRST_ORB_KILLS`): `_open_fight` passes what is left to `Encounter.orbs_after`, and `FightLedger.bank_kills` adds a fight's kills when it ends or the game closes.
- **No unique drops before the player's 100th kill** (`UniqueTable.FIRST_UNIQUE_KILLS`, passed as `Encounter.uniques_after`), **and the first boss to fall is promised one until one has been found** (`guarantee_unique`, from `inventory.uniques_found`), which the wait does not hold back.
- **The first elite is promised a drop until one has handed something over** (`inventory.first_elite_taken`, set by the ledger -- an autodiscarded elite drop counts).
- **Only the offensive stats, `bleed`, the three finders and the skill-only `orb_find` do anything in a fight:** `damage`, `crit_chance`, `crit_damage`, `attack_speed`, `bleed`, `drop_rate`, `item_rarity`, `gold_find`, `orb_find`, all read in `Encounter.arm()`. **Drop rate is the broad finder:** it lifts the gear roll and the unique roll, and in `_kill` it is **added** to `gold_find` for the purse and to `orb_find` for the orb roll, the way two global percents add. `gold_of` and `OrbTable.chance_for` themselves are untouched, so town prices -- quoted in bodies, with no player in them -- do not move. **`sight` is the live stat no fight reads:** `main_scene._sight()` asks the worn set for it where a won tile is charted (`_on_combat_finished`, and the dev skip-fight) and nowhere else. Capstone skills and worn uniques arrive together as `Encounter.effects` (`Inventory.effects()`). Every defensive stat is rolled, saved and shown and does nothing, because nothing can hurt the player yet -- **except through a unique**: armour feeds damage under the Spiked Helm, health buys clock under the Heartwood Plate, block is a second swing under the Bulwark.
- **Monsters outgrow gear on purpose.** `HP_GROWTH` is per hex step, `LEVEL_GROWTH` per level, and levels come in widening bands. `test_combat._test_a_won_fight` pins the three ends (first ring beatable bare-handed; a plain set of the plainest material at the far edge needs 8+ clicks a second, and in the best material the level unlocks still over 7; farmed rares bring it under 8 but over 1). The farmed reference is `_typical_farmed`, the median of `FARMED_SETS` seeded sets, never one set. Dials: `HP_GROWTH`, `LEVEL_GROWTH`, `LEVEL_FLAT`, `LootTable.TIER_POWER`, a kind's own `power`, `Encounter.SECONDS`.
- **The comparison beside an open bag item is against everything the swap would take off** (`Equipment.displaced_by` on `Equipment.sockets_for(item)[BagPage._socket_pick]`), which for a two-hander is two pieces, each with its own Unequip. The socket is the one Equip targets: the emptiest until the comparison's **Swap** (rings only) turns both to the other finger, and back to it with every new selection. **Hide** (a caret pointing at the bag) takes the whole sheet away for the session, doll or comparison alike -- the doll wears the same caret in its top-right corner -- (`_compare_hidden`, not saved) and leaves **Show** (the caret turned round, `_show_button`) against the bag's edge in its place. Both marks sit beside the heading, except at a counter, where they take a row under the rule because `SHOP_WORN_WIDTH` has no room for them. The stat block itself never shows deltas.
- **A bag square takes no mouse input** (`MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE`): the `ScrollContainer` must see every press to tell a drag from a click, so the clicked square is worked out from the cursor position.
- **Tip copy:** a short title-case title, then two or three plain sentences in the game's voice: what happened, then what to do, naming a button by its picture and corner. No system words ("pouch", "autodiscard"), no dashes, nothing Pixellari may not draw.
- **A town is one thing on screen, not two pages.** `TownPage` on the right and the bag in shop mode on the left open and close together, and the town's own cell (`_town_cell`) is what the bag is pointed at -- never `map.selected_cell`, which is still clickable behind the page. `_close_town` runs from `_open_fight` as well, so a town goes away with the left pages.
- **The one hand that moves a held piece's level and base stats is the fortress smith** (`Blacksmith`, reached from `TownPage`'s smith tab), and it works on what the **bag** has open -- never a worn piece, never one off a shelf -- which is the crafting rule the orb tray has always obeyed. Its cap is the ground's own ceiling, so no counter walks gear past the frontier.
- **Money has one place it is made, `TownPrices`, and one unit, bodies.** Anything that pays or charges asks there; nothing outside it writes a figure in gold. The purse it is quoted against is `Encounter.gold_at_steps`, which `base_gold` is also built on, so the tile's payout and the shop's prices cannot drift apart.
- **A town's counters are derived and a town's doings are written down.** `TownServices.services_for` is a pure function of tier, spot and world seed and may be asked twice; anything rolled -- a vendor's shelf (`VendorStock`) and a board's postings (`BountyBoard`) -- goes in `TownState` inside `inventory.towns`, keyed by **world spot** as `"x,y"`, because a purchase moves the purse, the bag and the town together and one save is one write.
- **Where a monster lives is only ever somewhere the player has seen, and only once a fortuneteller has been paid to say** (`BountyBoard.located`; a missing unique's ground likewise, `FortuneTeller.peeked`). A bounty row's swatches are the tile panel's own (`HexTileset.env_icon`) and its "Nearest" is `MapBuilder.nearest_env`, which never offers a cell under the fog, never a settlement, and measures from where the player stands rather than from the town that posted the work. A page's Show says which cell and nothing more: the main scene closes the pages, selects it and moves the camera, and the tile panel's Move here / Farm / Chart do the rest.
- **Gear thrown away pays `Inventory.salvage(item)` in all four places it can be thrown away** -- nothing unless the Rag and Bone Sack is worn: `_on_loot_autodiscarded` and `_on_drop_discarded` through `_pay_salvage` and `ledger.add_gold` (so a run pouches it), `BagPage._on_discard_pressed` and `_on_clear_level_pressed` straight to the purse. A full bag destroying a piece is not the player throwing it away and pays nothing.
- **`_open_fight` calls `fight.wear(inventory.effects())` before `fight.arm(inventory.stats())`:** `arm` reads some effects, and `wear` reshapes the lineup before the view is built.
- **Nothing calls `Inventory.add` where the bag may be full** except the paths that mean it: a drop, which is what `trim` and the autodiscard rules exist for. A purchase is refused instead, greyed with the reason, because `add` destroys the worst piece in a full bag to make room. **Equip is greyed the same way** where a two-hander would hand two pieces back into a bag with room for one (`Inventory.can_equip`).
- **A growing quantity is a whole-valued `float` and is written only through `BigNumber.format`** (`Scenes/big_number.gd`): gold, every price and reward, monster health and damage. Round with `roundf` / `maxf`, never `roundi` / `maxi`, which overflow at 9.2e18; print with `%s`, never `%d`. Below a million it reads as the whole number, from there as `1.23e6`. Levels, kills, counts, percents, experience and modifier values stay `int`. `round()` / `max()` return a Variant and will not parse under `:=`.
