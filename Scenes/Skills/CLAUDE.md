## Skills (`Scenes/Skills/`)
Every level past the first is one skill point, spent in passive trees. The points are **not stored**: the free points are `Skills.earned(level) - spent()`, so a save can never disagree with itself about how many there are.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `skill_tree.gd` (`SkillTree`) | The tables and rules, no state. `TREES` holds **Power**, **Fortune** and **Guard** (armour, dodge, block, time on hit), each ten skills in the one shape they were sketched in: a root, two side skills, a middle skill either side opens, then three chains of two (`row`/`col` on a 5x3 grid). A skill opens once **any** parent has a point **and** the tree already holds `POINTS_PER_ROW` (3) points per row down -- 3 for row 1, 12 for the capstones -- all inside `is_open`, so the locked icon, `why_not` ("Needs 12 points in Power") and `from_dict`'s refund all follow it. The bottom row's skills also carry an `effect` and its `effect_text`, which `SkillCard` shows in gold: `execute` (a blow leaving under 10% kills), `cleave` (overkill carries into the next enemy), `giant_slayer` (double damage on elites and bosses), `trophy` (elites and bosses always drop), `jackpot` (1 purse in 10 is fivefold), `transmute` (an orb falls twice 1 time in 4), `afterimage` (a dodge wins back up to 1 s of `wounds`), `shieldwall` (block doubled against elites and bosses) and `second_wind` (once a fight, the clock running out gives back 5 s). Depth is paid for twice: `max_rank` falls 5 / 3 / 3 / 2 / 1 down the rows (23 a tree) while what a point does grows and widens. Each skill has `flat` (added to the set before anything multiplies) and `percent` (increased, applied as its own multiplier). The Fortune stats -- `drop_rate`, `item_rarity`, `gold_find`, `orb_find` -- are percentages already, so its skills add points of them flat. `can_rank` and `why_not` are one rule written twice, the way `OrbTable`'s are. `respec_cost(level, spent)` is `RESPEC_GOLD * spent * RESPEC_GROWTH ^ (level - 1)`. **Overranks:** once every tree is full (`all_full`; `is_full(tree)`), a skill takes ranks past its `max_rank`, each adding its per-rank numbers again; the nth past the most costs `OVERRANK_WEIGHT[row] * n` (1 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 6 down the rows). `rank_cost(id, rank)` is the next rank's price and `points_for(id, rank)` their sum; `why_not` says `FULL` of a maxed skill while a tree has room. `describe(id, points)` writes a skill's lines |
| `skills.gd` (`Skills`) | What the player has learned: `ranks`, id -> points, only for skills with any. `rank_up`, `reset(tree)`, `spent(tree)`, `points(level)`, and `flat()` / `percent()`, the sums `Equipment.totals` takes. `spent` counts **points** (`SkillTree.points_for`), not ranks, and `points` is `earned - spent`. `effects()` is the learned capstones' effect ids, which the main scene hands to `Encounter.effects`. `from_dict(data, level)` prunes unknown ids and cuts a rank past the most down to it unless its tree is full, then **refunds every rank** if the result overspends the level or holds a point nothing leads to -- after a retune there is no honest way to guess which points the player would have kept |

## Rules and gotchas
- **Skills stack around the gear's globals, not into them.** `Equipment.totals(skill_flat, skill_percent)` adds the pieces, then the skill flats, then applies the gear's `GLOBAL` percents, then the skills' percents as a **separate** multiplier -- so a skill point of damage is scaled by a ring like the sword's own, and a ring's 10% and a skill's 10% make 21%. `totals()` with nothing passed is the set alone. Flat skill damage is large against early gear and shrinks as gear grows exponentially, which is why `test_combat` holds a **level's worth** of Power points at the edge to "helps, still wants clicking" and the whole tree only to "helps".
- **Overranks grow a capstone's numbers, never its effect:** `effects()` lists an effect once however many ranks hold it. A Reset clears a whole tree, overranks and all, and no skill goes past its most again until that tree is refilled; the other trees keep theirs. A world's transcension starts the level, and so the skills, over.
